The path from zero to a shop that runs in the background

Built for people who don't have unlimited hours. Every step is scoped to what's actually achievable with a real schedule.

01

Find the right product category based on what's actually selling

We don't start with what you feel like making — we start with what buyers are already searching for on Etsy. That research is what separates shops that sell from shops that sit.

02

Create your first product and set up the listing correctly

Title, tags, description, pricing, mockups — the listing is the sales page. We get this right from the start so your product actually gets found by the people looking for it.

03

Learn what your Etsy stats are telling you — and act on it

Most sellers ignore their analytics. We learn to read the data so you know which products to put energy into, which listings to adjust, and where your traffic is actually coming from.

04

Build the catalog one product at a time until income is consistent

One product isn't a business. But 15-20 validated products, each contributing something, adds up. We build the catalog at a pace that works with your life — not a sprint that burns you out.
