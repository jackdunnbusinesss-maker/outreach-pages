VSL Framework Guide (9-Step — for Alicia's team)

  1. Hook: What you can specifically do for them — lead with the outcome, not the intro.
  2. Credibility: Specific numbers — 131K trained, ABC News, grant wins documented.
  3. Opportunity Sell: Why AI income is the biggest business opportunity right now and why timing matters.
  4. Present Offer: What the program is and the price — upfront. This filters leads and builds trust.
  5. Handle Objections: Address "I'm not technical," "I've tried before," and "I don't have time."
  6. Qualify Audience: Who this is specifically for. Disqualify explicitly — makes everyone else more certain.
  7. Clear CTA: Book the call. One action. No alternatives.
  8. Testimonials: Real names, specific numbers, varied situations.
  9. Reiterate Value + Risk Removal: Remind them of what they're getting, what the guarantee is, and what happens if they book.
