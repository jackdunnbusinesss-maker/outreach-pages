The method

Here's how students go from zero to consistent income — step by step.

This is the Clear Process to Revenue. Not strategy. Steps.

1

Research the right niche before touching Canva

Find niches with real demand and low competition using a specific process. 30 minutes per product. This is where 90% of sellers get it wrong.

2

Create or source your first product correctly

For Etsy: use the research findings to inform your design, not your taste. For Whatnot: source inventory that already has a proven sales history on the platform.

3

Optimize your listing for search — not for aesthetics

Etsy search is the traffic engine. A well-researched product with a poorly optimized listing stays invisible. A 30-minute listing setup done correctly outperforms months of random posting.

4

For Whatnot: structure your show for momentum

Show structure, product pacing, and audience engagement are what turn a flat show into a compounding one. The algorithm rewards shows that hold attention.

5

Repeat the research process with what the data tells you

You're not guessing by month three. You're running a repeatable process across multiple products, with sales data telling you what to do next.
