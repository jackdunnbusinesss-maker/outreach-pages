Common questions

Do I need modeling experience to join?

No. The system is designed to work from zero — someone who wants to start modeling or who has done a few shoots with no real direction. Experience helps but isn't required to get full value here.

What curl types does this cover?

The program covers a range of textures from wavy to coily. Mia's hair care modules address different curl patterns specifically — not a one-size approach to "curly hair."

Is this only for women?

The majority of Mia's audience and content is women-focused, but the industry navigation and hair care content is applicable regardless of gender for anyone working in modeling or beauty.

What if I just want the hair content and not the modeling side?

The beauty enthusiast track is specifically for that. You get the full curly hair mastery curriculum without needing to engage with the modeling content.

What market is this for? Can I use this outside the US?

The industry content is primarily US-focused but the hair care and mindset modules are universal. The modeling frameworks apply to most major markets with minor adjustments.
