How it works

A Planning System Built for Real Life

The Weekly Reset is a 20-minute process you run at the start of every week. It front-loads every hard decision — what matters, what doesn't, what happens when things go sideways — so you're not making priority calls when you're already tired.

It doesn't require a perfect week. It's built to survive an imperfect one.

1

Clear the backlog

Get everything out of your head and onto paper. Not a task list — a full brain dump. This is the step most planning systems skip and why you carry mental weight all week.

2

Decide what actually matters this week

Not everything on your list. The 3 things that, if you do nothing else, mean the week was a success. This decision — made in advance, while you're calm — is the whole game.

3

Assign them to real time

Not "I'll do it Monday." Monday at 10am, for 90 minutes. Time-blocked before the week starts. Everything else is noise until those three things are done.

4

Set your sick week protocol

What happens when Tuesday blows up? You already know. One fallback task — the single most important thing to move forward if everything else gets derailed. The system bends. It doesn't break.
