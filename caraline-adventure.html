Do I need gym access or can I train at home?

Both are covered. There are tracks for gym-based training and home-based training — the body mechanics principles apply in both settings.

How is "curvy" defined here? Who is this for?

If you've ever felt like standard fitness programs weren't made for your body — whether that's a larger lower half, different proportions, postpartum changes, or just more curves than the fitness industry typically shows — this is for you.

What if I'm a complete beginner?

The body mechanics foundation is built for people who have never trained seriously. Understanding how your body works is even more valuable when you're starting out — it saves you months of doing things the wrong way.

Is the fit mom track only for postpartum women?

It's designed for postpartum recovery and the specific physical changes that come with pregnancy and childbirth, but moms who are years past postpartum and dealing with related physical changes also benefit from it.

How much time per week does this require?

The programs are structured for real life — not competitive athletes or people with unlimited time. 3–4 sessions per week is the sweet spot, and the sessions are designed to be efficient without cutting corners on results.
