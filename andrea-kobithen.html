It's a cohort-based program specifically for stay-at-home moms who want community, support, and structure for navigating this season of life — built around Andrea's experience and the real conversations her audience has been asking for.

How much time does it require each week?

It's designed for a SAHM's actual schedule. No hour-long Zoom calls during nap time or assignments due at 9am. The structure works around your day, not the other way around.

Is this for any stay-at-home mom, or a specific stage?

Whether you're in the thick of babies and toddlers or further along with school-aged kids, if you're home full-time and navigating what that looks like — this is for you.

Facebook groups are unstructured and often chaotic. The Collective has a real cohort format — everyone joins at the same time, goes through the same experience, and actually knows each other by the end.
