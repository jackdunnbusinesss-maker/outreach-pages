Questions before joining

Is this only for Coast Guard or other branches too?

Natalie's content is Coast Guard-specific, but many military moms from other branches have found the Playbook and Circle useful. The core challenges of military moves and solo parenting are universal.

We don't have a PCS coming up yet. Is this still worth joining?

Yes. Most members join the Circle for the community support year-round, not just during a move. The Playbook is there when you need it — some people read it early so they're never caught off guard.

Is the Circle active or one of those communities where nothing happens?

Natalie is in it regularly, and so are the members. The monthly live calls keep it active. It's not a Facebook group that shows up in your feed once and then disappears.

What's the cost?

Covered when you get started. The focus is making sure it's the right fit — reach out and Natalie will walk you through the details.
