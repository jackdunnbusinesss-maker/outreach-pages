Common questions

Do I have to be fully sober to join?

No. Some people come in already sober. Some are considering it. Some just know something needs to change. Where you are right now is the right place to start.

Is this a clinical or medically supervised program?

No. The Sober Mom Collective is a community and support space — not a medical program. If you have physical dependency concerns, please consult a doctor. This is for the emotional and social support side of the journey.

I'm a single mom with almost no time. Is this realistic?

That's exactly who this was built for. You can engage as much or as little as your life allows. Even showing up to scroll through the community on a hard night counts. You don't have to be all-in to get something out of this.

How is this different from a Facebook group?

Abbey is in here. The tone is curated and consistent. The content is structured and intentional. This isn't a random group — it's a community built around a shared experience with actual leadership behind it.

What if I relapse while in the community?

You stay. That's the whole point. The collective is not a place that kicks you out when things get messy. It's a place that exists for exactly that moment.

You don't have to do this alone.

The hard part isn't quitting. It's staying quit without anyone around who actually understands. That's what the Sober Mom Collective is for.
