Is the Guide for international travel or domestic trips?

Both. Maria's covered family travel across different types of trips. The Guide covers the frameworks and logistics that apply whether you're driving two hours or flying somewhere new.

What ages does the Guide work for?

Families with kids from toddler age through elementary school. Maria's traveled through multiple stages with her own family and the Guide reflects that range.

Is this a budget travel guide?

It's a realistic family travel guide. It covers how to make smart decisions at different budget levels — not luxury content, not extreme frugality. Real options for real working families.

I barely have time to read. Is this actually usable?

It's designed for working moms who don't have time to wade through content. Organized by trip stage, scannable, and built to be referenced — not read cover to cover once and forgotten.

Free content covers destinations. The Guide covers logistics — the stuff that actually makes or breaks a family trip. Packing with kids, managing travel days, what to do when things go sideways. The real part.
