How it works

The exact process from zero to a working 6-figure Etsy digital product business

Yale did this herself. Here's the process, step by step.

01

Find the right niche using real Etsy data — not guessing

We use the same analysis Yale applies to the 100+ shops she's studied. Revenue data, sell-through rates, competition level. You pick a niche based on what's actually selling — not what sounds interesting.

02

Make a digital product in under an hour that matches validated demand

Not six months of building. You identify a product that fits the market gap, create it fast, and launch to test. If it sells, you build more. If it doesn't, you iterate without wasting months.

03

Optimize the listing for Etsy SEO and conversion

The product is only part of it. The title, tags, description, thumbnail, and pricing all affect where Etsy puts you in search results and whether visitors buy. We fix each one specifically.

04

Scale by adding products and running targeted Etsy Ads

Once a product is converting, you scale it. More products in adjacent categories, strategic Etsy Ads on proven listings, and the listing optimization loop that keeps growing your share of the search results.
