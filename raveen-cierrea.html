The Real Problem

Where Most Women Get Stuck

Relaxed hair is its own science. Treat it like natural hair and you'll lose progress. Treat it without intention and you'll lose more. Here's where most women go wrong.

🧴

Relaxer day that damages everything

Touch-ups feel like roulette. Sometimes the result is great, sometimes you lose ends — and you don't know what makes the difference.

💧

Dryness that nothing fixes

Your hair drinks moisture and still feels brittle by day three. The leave-ins aren't the problem — the foundation is.

✂️

Length that never compounds

You grow it, you lose it. Trims, breakage, and setbacks reset your progress every season.

🌀

Conflicting advice everywhere

Half the internet says protein. Half says moisture. Nobody tells you when, how often, or how to read your own hair.

🔥

Heat damage you can't undo

One bad heat session sets you back six months. The line between styled and damaged is thinner than anyone admits.
