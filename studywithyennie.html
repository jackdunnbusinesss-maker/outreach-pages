How it works

A daily habit that builds on itself, not one that resets every week.

The accountability program is built around the same structure I use every day in my study sessions. Here's exactly what changes and in what order:

01

Week 1-2: Build the baseline

Get a study system that's designed to function on your worst days, not just your best ones. Establish the daily trigger, the session structure, and the review habit that makes everything else work.

02

Week 3-4: Stop losing ground

Implement a semester-level plan that spreads the load so you're never cramming. Know exactly where you are at any point in the semester and what needs to happen before exams.

03

Month 2: The routine becomes normal

By this point, most students say the system feels like what they do — not something they have to force. The accountability sessions become the anchor the rest of the day organizes around.

04

End of semester: Show up to exams prepared

Not cramming, not panicking. You've been reviewing material at the pace it's been delivered. The exam is the final step, not the first time you've actually engaged with the content.
