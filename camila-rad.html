Common questions

Is this for beginners or experienced lifters?

Both. Beginners benefit from building recovery habits from the start. Experienced lifters benefit because recovery is usually the most neglected part of a serious training routine — it becomes more important, not less, as your training intensity increases.

Does this replace my training program?

No. The Recovery Reset runs alongside whatever you're already doing in the gym. You bring your training — this program handles everything that supports and amplifies it.

Is this mostly about sleep or does it cover everything?

Sleep is one of five major modules. The program covers sleep, stress, nutrition for recovery, mental health, and active recovery protocols — all of which interact and all of which matter.

I have a busy schedule. How demanding is this program?

The point of the program is sustainability. It's not designed to add a second full-time job to your life — it's designed to integrate into your existing routine in a way that gradually shifts your baseline recovery quality.

Is the mental health content clinical or conversational?

Conversational. Camila isn't a therapist and the program isn't a clinical intervention — it's practical guidance for understanding how psychological patterns affect physical performance, delivered the way she delivers everything: honestly and without judgment.
