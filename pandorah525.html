Questions

Is this only for nurses, or does it work for other healthcare workers?

The system was built with nurses in mind but works for any shift worker in healthcare — CNAs, techs, paramedics, anyone running a rotating schedule with long hours.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Most workouts in the system are home-based. There's an optional gym track if you prefer that, but it's not required.

What if I work nights? Is the nutrition guidance different?

Yes — there's a dedicated night shift nutrition module. The timing of meals and the types of foods that work at 2am are completely different from daytime eating.

How much time do the workouts take?

Most sessions run 20–30 minutes. They're designed for your off days — not your post-shift days when you have nothing left in the tank.
