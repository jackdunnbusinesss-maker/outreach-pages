Areas of Expertise

  • Construction Claims & Dispute Management

    Strategic management of construction claims from early identification through to final resolution — whether through negotiation, adjudication, arbitration, or litigation. We help parties understand their position and pursue it effectively.

  • Expert Witness Services

    Highly credentialed expert witnesses providing independent opinions on technical, financial, and industry-specific matters in Australian courts, international arbitral tribunals, and regulatory proceedings.

    Comprehensive forensic and financial analysis to support parties in commercial litigation — including loss quantification, contract interpretation support, and document review and analysis.
