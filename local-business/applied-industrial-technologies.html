The components and solutions that keep industry moving

Applied Industrial Technologies is a leading distributor of industrial bearings, power transmission components, fluid power systems, and engineered solutions. Serving manufacturing, mining, food processing, and other industries, Applied helps customers reduce downtime and improve operational efficiency.

Applied Industrial Technologies has built its reputation as one of the most trusted names in industrial distribution across North America and beyond. The company stocks and supplies a broad range of mechanical components sourced from leading manufacturers, giving maintenance teams and procurement managers access to the parts they need without unnecessary lead times.

Beyond pure distribution, Applied offers engineered solutions that help customers solve problems rather than simply replace parts. Technical specialists work alongside plant engineers and maintenance teams to identify inefficiencies, recommend the right products, and support installation and commissioning. That consultative model means Applied is as useful at the design stage as it is during an unplanned shutdown.

Applied serves a wide cross-section of industries including manufacturing, food and beverage processing, mining, aggregate, rubber and plastics, and primary metals. Wherever rotating equipment, conveying systems, or hydraulic and pneumatic machinery is in use, Applied has the products, the expertise, and the logistics network to keep operations running.

What we supply and solve

  • Industrial Bearings

    A comprehensive range of ball bearings, roller bearings, housed units, and specialist bearings from leading global manufacturers, available for immediate supply or scheduled delivery to suit maintenance programmes.

  • Power Transmission Components

    Belts, pulleys, gearboxes, couplings, chain drives, and related mechanical power transmission products, stocked in depth for both maintenance replacements and new equipment builds.

  • Fluid Power Systems

    Hydraulic and pneumatic components including pumps, cylinders, valves, hose assemblies, and filtration equipment, with system design and troubleshooting support available from specialist fluid power engineers.

  • Engineered Solutions

    Technical advisory and engineered solutions services for manufacturing, food processing, mining, and other industries, focused on improving asset reliability, reducing unplanned downtime, and lowering total cost of maintenance.
