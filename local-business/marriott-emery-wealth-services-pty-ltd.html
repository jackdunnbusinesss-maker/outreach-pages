About Marriott Emery Wealth Services

Based at 140 George St, Bundaberg West QLD 4670, Marriott Emery Wealth Services Pty Ltd (ABN 53 137 727 176) trades as Lifestyle Mortgage Finance & Lifestyle Financial Planning. We are an Authorised Representative and Credit Representative of Akumin Financial Planning Pty Limited.

We believe that good financial advice isn't exclusive to the wealthy. Every Australian has the potential to build financial independence — and a skilled adviser can help you put thought into action, especially when life gets in the way of planning ahead.

Our team develops tailored, step-by-step financial strategies and helps clients make informed decisions at any stage of life. You work hard — we make sure your money works just as hard for you.

Our services

  • Superannuation

    Build your retirement savings strategically while you work. We help you understand your super options and ensure you're set up for a comfortable future.

  • Retirement Planning

    Retirement may seem a long way off, but investing in your future today is a tax-effective strategy that pays dividends when it matters most.

  • Investments

    Everyone has different needs and circumstances. We work with you to identify the investment approach that fits your goals before you put a dollar to work.

  • Wealth Protection

    Insurance is the foundation of any sound financial plan. We evaluate your risks and recommend the right protection for you and your family.

    Smart debt strategies that reduce your financial burden and free up capital to build the future you want.

  • Estate Planning

    Protect what you've built and ensure your wealth is transferred to the people you care about — clearly, efficiently, and on your terms.

  • Aged Care

    Navigate the financial complexities of aged care with confidence. We help families plan for this stage with clarity and compassion.
