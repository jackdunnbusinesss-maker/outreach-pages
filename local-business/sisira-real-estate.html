About Sisira Real Estate

Since 2010, Sisira Real Estate has been a dedicated property agency serving Melbourne's northern growth corridor. We work across residential sales, rentals, and property management — giving buyers, sellers, and renters the local expertise they need to move with confidence.

Our portfolio spans sought-after suburbs including Craigieburn, Roxburgh Park, Mernda, and Broadford — areas experiencing strong demand and continued development. Whether you're looking for your first home, an investment property, or a rental, we match people with properties that fit.

Our team handles every stage of the transaction — from initial listing through to settlement — with clear communication and hands-on support at every step.
