Australia's Energy Infrastructure

Connecting Australia with energy infrastructure

APA Group is one of Australia's leading energy infrastructure businesses, owning and operating a diverse portfolio of gas transmission pipelines, gas storage and processing facilities, and renewable energy assets across the country.

APA Group is a critical part of Australia's energy infrastructure, operating the pipelines and facilities that move and store energy across the continent. The group's transmission network connects producers to markets and industries, underpinning the energy supply chains that households and businesses depend on.

Beyond gas transmission, APA is an active participant in Australia's energy transition — investing in and operating renewable energy assets that complement the existing infrastructure portfolio. The business is positioned to support Australia's shift to a lower-carbon energy system while continuing to deliver the reliable energy supply the economy needs today.

APA operates offices across NSW, QLD, VIC, WA, and the NT, with a national team focused on safe, reliable, and responsible operation of energy infrastructure that matters to every Australian.

What APA Group operates

  • Gas Transmission
    A national network of gas transmission pipelines that connect gas producers to downstream markets, industrial customers, and gas-fired power generation across Australia.
  • Gas Storage & Processing
    Gas storage and processing facilities that provide flexibility and security in Australia's gas supply system — ensuring reliable delivery even as production profiles and demand fluctuate.
  • Renewable Energy
    Renewable energy assets that form a growing part of APA's portfolio, supporting Australia's energy transition and providing the clean energy capacity the grid will increasingly require.
  • National Operations
    Operations teams across NSW, QLD, VIC, WA, and the NT maintaining and operating infrastructure safely and reliably — backed by APA's commitment to responsible energy delivery.
