About EW&L

Over the past 30 years, Emanuel Whybourne & Loehr has positively changed the lives of more than 200 HNW families. Our clients range from founders and creators of some of Australia's largest businesses to retired executives, not-for-profits, and foundations.

We are wealth advisors who assist Australian business owners, professionals, and investors who value strategic, long-term investment advice — advice that builds wealth now and secures their family's future. Our solutions are not limited to any one firm, geography, asset class, or corporate interest. We simply find the best solutions for your needs.

Based at Level 3, The Eminence, Fortitude Valley QLD, our Brisbane team works closely with clients across the eastern seaboard to deliver responsive, personal service at every stage of the wealth journey.
