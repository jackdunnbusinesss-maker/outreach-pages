5-minute strategy walkthrough — SA and international MICE market

TIME is a South Africa-based full-service MICE event management company — handling meetings, incentives, conferences, and exhibitions across South Africa's world-class event destinations. Cape Town, the Winelands, the Kruger, and Johannesburg give TIME access to a MICE product that international corporate buyers consistently rate as among the best value and most experientially differentiated in the world. The challenge is getting in front of those buyers before they default to a European or Asian destination out of habit.

International corporate event planners in the UK, Australia, and UAE are actively evaluating South Africa as an incentive destination — but they need a local partner they trust before they commit the programme. South African corporate event managers are sourcing MICE partners for conferences and exhibitions and often defaulting to whoever they've used before. The strategy we've built for TIME covers both markets — with LinkedIn for B2B outreach, Google for search capture, and destination content that does the hard work of putting South Africa at the top of the shortlist.
