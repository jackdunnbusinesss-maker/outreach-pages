Move people, food and freight on demand

Uber connects riders, drivers, restaurants and businesses across 70 or more countries through a single platform built for reliability, speed and scale.

Getting from A to B, or getting dinner from a favourite restaurant to your door, should be straightforward. Uber has spent over a decade building the infrastructure, technology and driver networks to make that happen at city scale, in real time, across Australia and around the world.

In Australia, Uber operates in every major metro market. Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth each have mature driver networks running around the clock. Whether that is a standard UberX trip across the city, a premium ride via Uber Black, or a scheduled airport transfer through Uber Reserve, the platform is built to handle the full range of passenger needs.

Beyond passenger transport, Uber Eats has become one of Australia's most-used food delivery platforms, connecting thousands of restaurants to customers at home or at work. Uber Freight extends the same on-demand model to logistics, while Uber for Business gives companies a managed travel and meals solution that replaces fragmented expense processes.

What the platform offers

  • Uber Rides

    On-demand passenger transport across UberX, Uber Comfort, Uber Black and more. Available in Australian capital cities with real-time driver matching and upfront pricing.

  • Uber Reserve

    Scheduled rides booked in advance, with a confirmed driver assigned ahead of time. Ideal for airport transfers, early departures and time-sensitive commitments.

  • Uber Eats

    Food and grocery delivery from thousands of local restaurants and stores, tracked in real time from order confirmation to your door.

  • Uber for Business & Freight

    Centralised travel and meals management for organisations, plus on-demand freight matching for carriers and shippers moving goods at scale.
