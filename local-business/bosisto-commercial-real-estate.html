About Bosisto Commercial

Bosisto Commercial Real Estate is a boutique commercial agency focused on creating new commercial projects that cater to the rapidly expanding small to medium business sector across metropolitan Melbourne.

Based at 1/33 Fennell St, Port Melbourne VIC 3207, the team has spent over 30 years specialising in the project marketing and sales of strata office buildings — delivering results for vendors, landlords, and growing businesses that need the right commercial space.

From office and showroom suites through to high-clearance warehouses and logistics precincts, Bosisto matches businesses with properties suited to how they actually operate — not just what's available.
