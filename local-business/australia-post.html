About Us

Serving Australia for generations

Australia Post provides postal, retail, financial and travel services for personal, business, enterprise and government customers across Australia. With one of the country's largest retail networks, we connect communities and power commerce from the corner store to the global marketplace.

Whether you're sending a parcel next door, exchanging currency before a holiday, or managing high-volume business mail, Australia Post has the infrastructure, expertise and reach to get it done reliably and affordably.
