Blackstone manages over $1 trillion in AUM across real estate, private equity, hedge fund solutions, and credit and insurance — the world's largest alternative asset manager by AUM. The institutional relationships are established. The brand is unambiguous at the institutional level. The growth opportunity is in the broader addressable market: the RIA channel, the high-net-worth individual seeking alternatives access, and the financial adviser who is increasingly allocating client capital to alternatives for the first time.

Alternatives demand is growing faster than advisor familiarity. The advisers and HNWIs who are evaluating alternatives for the first time are searching, reading, and asking questions before they call. The strategy we've built for Blackstone covers how to capture that demand — across paid search, LinkedIn thought leadership, and content that educates at the point of research and converts at the point of allocation decision.

The walkthrough above covers all three audience segments: institutional, RIA/financial adviser, and HNW individual — with different content tracks for each.

What we'd build for Blackstone

  • Institutional Thought Leadership
    LinkedIn Sponsored Content delivering Blackstone research and market perspectives to pension fund allocators, endowments, and sovereign wealth managers globally — positioning Blackstone's investment thesis at the point of annual allocation review.
  • RIA and Financial Adviser Channel
    Paid media and content targeting registered investment advisers and independent financial planners who are actively increasing alternatives allocations — specifically Blackstone's BREIT, BCRED, and BXPE structures for accredited investor clients.
  • HNW Individual Acquisition
    Facebook and Google targeting high-net-worth individuals (household income $500K+, investable assets $5M+) researching alternative investments — education-first content that positions Blackstone as the authoritative alternatives resource.
  • Competitive Positioning
    Search and content strategy that captures the comparison research between Blackstone and KKR, Apollo, and Carlyle — ensuring Blackstone's case is made before allocation decisions narrow to a shortlist.
