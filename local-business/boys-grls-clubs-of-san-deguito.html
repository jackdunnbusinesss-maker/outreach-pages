5-minute strategy walkthrough — Encinitas and Carlsbad focus

Boys & Girls Clubs of Northwest San Diego serves youth aged 6–18 across Encinitas and Carlsbad through after-school programmes, aquatics, summer camps, and the ROADS teen leadership track. For working families in North County, BGC fills a real gap — supervised, structured, developmental programming at an accessible price point. The programmes are working. The challenge is that not enough families in the area know they exist.

Parents searching for after-school care in Encinitas or Carlsbad, and donors looking for a local youth charity to support, are finding competitors and national organisations before they find BGC. The strategy we've built for BGC Northwest San Diego covers three simultaneous goals: enrolment growth, donor acquisition, and volunteer recruitment — with paid media, Google Business Profile, and local search capture working together.

The walkthrough above shows what a 90-day digital campaign looks like across all three conversion tracks.

What we'd build for BGC NW San Diego

  • Family Enrolment
    Google Search and Facebook campaigns targeting parents of children 6–18 in Encinitas, Carlsbad, Solana Beach, and Del Mar — "after school care Encinitas", "youth programmes Carlsbad", "summer camp North County San Diego" — driving enquiry form submissions.
  • Donor Acquisition
    Facebook impact content campaign targeting North County adults 30–65 with charitable interests — specific programme outcomes, community footprint, and a clear donation CTA with tax-deductible messaging. Local giving, visible impact.
  • Volunteer Pipeline
    Facebook and Instagram targeting adults 22–45 in North County with mentoring and community interests — specific volunteer roles, time commitments, and the tangible difference each shift makes. Volunteer application conversion CTA.
  • Google Business Profile
    Separate optimised GBP listings for Encinitas and Carlsbad — photos, services, hours, and a review strategy that builds the visible community trust parents need before they enrol their children.
