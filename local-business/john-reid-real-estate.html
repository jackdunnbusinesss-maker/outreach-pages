About John Reid Real Estate

For 47 years, John Reid Real Estate has been the Gold Coast's most enduring prestige property agency. Three generations of family ownership have built a reputation on deep local knowledge, an unwavering focus on service, and a client base that returns — not because they have to, but because they choose to.

The agency operates from 67 Rio Vista Boulevard, Broadbeach Waters — at the heart of the Gold Coast prestige market. Unlike larger firms chasing volume, John Reid keeps its focus narrow and its standard high. Boutique by design. Record-breaking by result.

The foundation of the business has never been gimmicks or self-promotion. It's been expertise, market knowledge, and a genuine connection to the community. Clients do the boasting — so the agency doesn't need to.
