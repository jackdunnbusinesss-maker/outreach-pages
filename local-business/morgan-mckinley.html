Finance and technology talent, placed with global perspective

Morgan McKinley is a permanent and contract recruitment agency specialising in finance and technology. With a global reach and a local presence in Sydney, the team delivers executive search, talent solutions, and flexible workforce models for businesses that need to hire well.

Morgan McKinley operates internationally, bringing a global perspective with a local touch to every placement. The firm focuses on the finance and technology sectors, where the quality of a hire has a direct and measurable impact on business performance. That sector focus shapes the depth of Morgan McKinley's candidate networks and the quality of its client advice.

The approach is built around future-proof talent strategies. Rather than simply filling vacancies, the team works to understand how a business is changing and what kind of people it will need in six, twelve, and twenty-four months. Whether that means placing a single permanent hire, building a contract team, or restructuring an entire recruitment function through an outsourced model, Morgan McKinley has the capability to deliver at each level.

Clients include businesses of varying size across financial services, fintech, and technology who need consistent access to quality candidates. Candidates benefit from a team that understands their market and can represent them accurately to employers. The Sydney office serves clients and candidates across the broader Australian market.
