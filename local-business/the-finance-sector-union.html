About the Finance Sector Union

The Finance Sector Union has been advocating and supporting finance workers across Australia for over 100 years. We are the union for employees in banking, insurance, finance, and related industries — people who keep the financial system running but too often face insecurity, unfair treatment, and inadequate protections.

We campaign for better pay, conditions, and dignity in the workplace — and we don't just campaign, we win. From protecting bank branches to fighting the unregulated rollout of AI, the FSU is at the frontline of every major issue facing finance workers today.

What the FSU Does for Members

  • Member Benefits

    Exclusive access to a wide range of member benefits, discounts, and support services designed specifically for finance sector workers.

  • Workplace Campaigns

    Coordinated campaigns to improve wages, working conditions, and job security for finance sector employees across Australia.

  • AI in Finance Advocacy

    Actively challenging the unregulated introduction of AI in banking and finance — ensuring technology is deployed fairly and transparently, without destroying jobs.

  • Gender Equity Campaigns

    Fighting for equal pay, parental leave, flexible work arrangements, and an end to discrimination for women in the finance sector.

  • Save Our Bank Branches

    Campaigning to protect physical bank branches and the jobs that come with them — a vital service for communities across Australia.

  • Legal Support & Advice

    Access to expert legal advice and representation for members facing workplace disputes, unfair dismissal, or discrimination.

  • Journey Insurance Protection

    Complimentary journey accident insurance for members travelling to and from work — an FSU member benefit.

  • Exclusive Member Discounts

    Significant savings on health insurance, travel, technology, entertainment, and more — reserved exclusively for FSU members.
