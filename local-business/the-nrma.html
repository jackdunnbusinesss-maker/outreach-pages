On the road, on the water, we have you covered

The NRMA provides Roadside Assistance as well as Car Batteries, Member benefits and discounts on Car Insurance, Fuel, Accommodation and more.

There are moments on the road where everything stops. A flat battery, a blown tyre, an engine that will not turn over. These are the moments The NRMA was built for. As a membership organisation with deep roots in New South Wales and the ACT, the NRMA has been providing roadside assistance to Australian motorists for generations, with a network that now reaches across the country.

Membership goes further than a call-out in an emergency. NRMA members access discounts and benefits across fuel, accommodation, vehicle-related services and more. The NRMA Group has also grown into a broader travel and tourism business, owning and operating NRMA Parks and Resorts, a SIXT car rental network and several Tasmanian tourism attractions, giving members more ways to get value from the organisation year-round.

Whether you are planning a family road trip, need a reliable battery replacement, or want to explore Australia with the confidence of knowing help is available when you need it, NRMA membership is the foundation that makes it possible.

What NRMA membership includes

  • Roadside Assistance

    24/7 breakdown support across Australia, including battery jump-starts, flat tyre changes, emergency fuel delivery, lockout assistance and towing where required.

  • Car Batteries

    On-site battery testing and replacement by NRMA technicians, with quality batteries fitted at your location so you are back moving without a trip to a workshop.

  • Member Benefits & Discounts

    Savings on fuel, accommodation, car insurance and more, with an ongoing program of member-only offers across travel, retail and vehicle services.

  • Parks, Resorts & Vehicle Rental

    Access to NRMA Parks and Resorts holiday parks, SIXT car and vehicle rental, and Tasmanian tourism attractions, with member pricing available across the portfolio.
