Auckland's water, managed with care

Watercare Services Limited delivers safe, reliable drinking water and responsible wastewater management to the people of Auckland every single day.

Water is not a commodity. It is a shared resource that sustains every household, business and ecosystem across the Auckland region. Watercare Services Limited exists to manage that resource on behalf of the community, with accountability and long-term thinking at the centre of every decision.

From source to tap and back again, the full water cycle sits under Watercare's stewardship. That means maintaining the infrastructure that moves water across one of Australasia's fastest-growing cities, detecting and fixing leaks before they become crises, and treating wastewater to the environmental standards Auckland's harbours and streams deserve.

If you think your property might have a leak, the impact goes further than your water bill. Every litre lost is a drain on a shared system. Watercare provides tools and guidance to help customers identify and resolve leaks quickly, protecting both individual properties and the broader network.

What Watercare does

  • Water Supply

    Safe, treated drinking water delivered reliably to homes and businesses across the Auckland region, sourced and managed with long-term environmental stewardship in mind.

  • Wastewater Services

    Collection, treatment and responsible discharge of wastewater, protecting Auckland's waterways, coastal environments and public health.

  • Infrastructure Management

    Ongoing maintenance and capital investment in the pipes, pumping stations, treatment plants and reservoirs that keep water moving safely across the region.

  • Leak Detection

    Proactive identification and repair of leaks across the network, alongside customer support for identifying issues within private properties before they escalate.
