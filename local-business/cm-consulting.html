Training and coaching that builds capable organisations

CM Consulting is an Australian professional training and coaching practice. The team works with organisations and individuals to strengthen workplace capability through coaching, leadership development, and professional training engagements.

CM Consulting operates in the professional training and coaching space, supporting organisations that want to develop the capability of their people. The practice works with clients across a range of industries, delivering training and coaching engagements tailored to the needs of the business and the individuals within it.

The consulting approach is grounded in practical outcomes. Rather than off-the-shelf programs, CM Consulting focuses on understanding the specific context and objectives of each client before designing an appropriate engagement, whether that involves individual coaching, team-based training, or a broader workplace consulting scope.

CM Consulting serves clients across Australia, working with businesses, teams, and individual professionals who are looking to build the skills and behaviours that lead to better workplace outcomes.

How CM Consulting works with clients

  • Professional Training

    Structured training engagements delivered to teams and organisations, designed to build specific professional skills and capabilities relevant to the client's industry and context.

  • Coaching

    One-on-one and group coaching for professionals and leaders, focused on helping individuals develop their effectiveness, decision-making, and professional presence.

  • Leadership Development

    Engagements focused on building leadership capability within organisations, supporting managers and leaders to perform more effectively in their roles.

  • Workplace Consulting

    Broader consulting support for organisations navigating workplace challenges, culture considerations, or capability gaps that require a structured consulting approach.
