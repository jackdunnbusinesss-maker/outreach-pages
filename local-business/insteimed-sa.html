Lithium Production — From Earth to Energy

Pioneering the future of lithium extraction

Lithium Argentina AG operates and accelerates the development of low-cost lithium projects through innovation, responsibility, collaboration and strategic partnerships — positioned at the centre of the global energy transition.

Argentina's vast lithium resources are critical to meeting surging global demand, driven primarily by electric vehicles and large-scale energy storage systems. Lithium Argentina AG is positioned to be a key supplier of high-quality, cost-effective lithium to meet those needs — with operations already in production and further projects in active development.

The company's strategic approach combines fundamental scientific first principles with modern technologies to better target resources and make efficient investments. Lithium Argentina bridges different disciplines and cultures to get the best perspectives and contributions — recognising that innovation in extraction requires a diversity of thinking, not just technical expertise.

With a clear focus on brine pond optimisation and scalable low-cost production, the company integrates conventional and emerging technologies to continuously improve lithium recovery across its operations.

Operations & projects

  • Cauchari–Olaroz — In Production
    Argentina's largest lithium brine operation and the fourth largest measured and indicated lithium resource globally. A flagship asset producing lithium at scale from the Jujuy province of northwest Argentina.
  • Pozuelos–Pastos Grandes — In Development
    A multi-phase growth opportunity integrating conventional and emerging technologies from multiple projects to become one of the latest lithium projects globally. Designed from the ground up for scalable, low-cost production.
  • Pioneering Exploration
    Combining fundamental scientific first principles with modern technologies to better target resources and make efficient investments — reducing exploration risk and accelerating development timelines.
  • Brine Pond Optimisation
    Enhancing lithium recovery through improved system design and process efficiency — integrating conventional and emerging technologies to support scalable, low-cost lithium production across the company's portfolio.
