About Newton Real Estate

Since 1986, Newton Real Estate has been helping Australians navigate property with confidence. Led by David Newton, the consultancy is built on a single principle: honest, personalised advice that puts your interests first — not the deal.

Whether you're buying your first home, managing an investment portfolio, navigating a tenancy dispute, or deciding when to sell, Newton Real Estate brings over 35 years of deep industry knowledge and strong connections across all areas and sectors of the real estate market.

We're familiar with current real estate trends, we understand local dynamics, and we give you the full picture — so you can make the right decision, at the right time, with complete confidence.

Our solutions

  • Property Advice

    Comprehensive, unbiased guidance for buyers, sellers, landlords and tenants — covering strategy, timing, negotiation, and market positioning at every stage of the property journey.

  • Rent Reviews

    Independent rent assessments to ensure landlords are achieving fair market rates and tenants understand their obligations — clear, data-backed, and impartial.

  • Investments & Home Loans

    Strategic advice on investment property selection and access to strong connections in home loan services — helping you structure your property portfolio for long-term performance.

  • Selling Advice

    Tailored guidance on when and how to sell — including pricing strategy, agent selection, and preparing your property to maximise your outcome in any market condition.
