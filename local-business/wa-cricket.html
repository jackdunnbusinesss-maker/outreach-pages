Governing and growing cricket across Western Australia

WA Cricket, the Western Australian Cricket Association, is the governing body of cricket in Western Australia. The organisation oversees elite squads, club cricket, membership, and the development of the game at every level across the state.

The Western Australian Cricket Association has long served as the governing body for cricket in Western Australia, responsible for the sport from elite competition through to grassroots participation. As the organisation overseeing WA's pathway into national cricket, WACA plays a central role in how the game is structured, supported, and grown across the state.

WA Cricket fields both men's and women's representative squads competing at the national level, as well as the WA Women's Aboriginal Team, which reflects the organisation's commitment to broad participation and the recognition of cricket's place in diverse communities across the state. Alongside elite competition, the association supports club cricket throughout Western Australia, providing the infrastructure that community clubs rely on to operate.

Cricket development sits at the heart of what WA Cricket does, connecting aspiring players and coaches to pathways that can take them from local clubs through to state and national representation. Memberships are available to supporters who want to follow the WA squads throughout the season. The reception team is available on weekdays between 9am and 4:30pm, and during match days.

What WA Cricket oversees

  • Elite Squads

    WA Cricket fields the WA Men's Squad and WA Women's Squad in national competition, as well as the WA Women's Aboriginal Team, providing elite pathways for Western Australian cricketers at every level.

  • Membership

    WA Cricket memberships give supporters access to matches and connect them directly to the state's cricketing community throughout the season.

  • Cricket Development

    Development programmes create structured pathways from junior and community cricket through to representative and elite levels, building the pipeline of players and coaches that WA cricket depends on.

  • Club Cricket

    WA Cricket supports and oversees club cricket across Western Australia, providing the governance, competitions, and resources that keep community clubs active and thriving throughout the state.
