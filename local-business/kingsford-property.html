About Kingsford Property

Since 1996, Kingsford Property has been one of Melbourne's most trusted real estate agencies — serving buyers, sellers, rental providers, and renters with professional, personalised service across the full property lifecycle.

From inner-city apartments on Little Bourke Street to serviced accommodation across Melbourne CBD and surrounding suburbs, the Kingsford team applies deep market knowledge and genuine care to every transaction. Our approach is built on clear communication, honest advice, and delivering the best possible results for every client.

We cover the full spread of Melbourne — from Abbotsford and Albert Park to St Kilda, South Yarra, Southbank, Port Melbourne, and beyond — giving clients access to one of the city's most comprehensive local property networks.
