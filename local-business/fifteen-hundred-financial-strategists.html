The 6 Steps

What to expect when you work with us.

No mystery about what happens after the first call. Every client moves through the same structured process — so the advice you get is considered, documented, and properly reviewed.

01

Initial meeting

A free, no-obligation chat. We listen to where you're at, what's on your mind, and what you're trying to achieve. Most first meetings run 45–60 minutes.

02

Fact find

The detailed picture — income, assets, liabilities, super, insurance, dependents, timeframes. Whatever's relevant. We can't build a plan on half the story.

03

Strategy development

Back at our desk, we model the options and build the strategy. This is where the actual planning happens — it's not done in front of you.

04

Strategy presentation

We walk you through the plan. Every recommendation, every why, every cost. You get a Statement of Advice to take away and review.

05

Implementation

You give the green light; we do the paperwork. Super rollovers, insurance applications, investment setups, estate planning referrals — handled.

06

Ongoing review

Life changes. Markets change. Rules change. We review the plan with you annually to make sure it's still doing what it's meant to.
