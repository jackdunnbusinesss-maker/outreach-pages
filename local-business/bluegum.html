About Bluegum

A Melbourne original, built on responsible manufacturing

Bluegum was born in Melbourne, Australia, and has grown into one of the country's most trusted names in wholesale apparel and promotional merchandise. Since 2006, we've been supplying retailers, corporates, and event organisers with ethically produced garments and branded product that genuinely reflects the values of the businesses that carry them.

We hold Ethical Clothing Australia (ECA) accreditation — a distinction earned by only a handful of Australian garment manufacturers. That accreditation reflects our commitment to responsible manufacturing practices: fair working conditions, premium materials, and a supply chain that respects both the people who make our products and the environment they're made in.

Whether you need a single custom range or ongoing wholesale supply across multiple product categories, our end-to-end service handles everything from design brief to delivery — no handoffs, no uncertainty, no compromise on quality.
