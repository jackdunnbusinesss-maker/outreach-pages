Excavators, skid steers, tipper trucks, compaction rollers and more. RAM Equipment supplies dry hire and sales to construction crews across South Australia, from Adelaide to Whyalla and Olympic Dam.

RAM Equipment is Adelaide's dry hire specialist for construction and civil works. With depots at Richmond SA, Whyalla, and Olympic Dam, the fleet is positioned to service projects across the state without the delays that come with sourcing from interstate. Every machine goes out clean, serviced, and ready to work.

The hire range covers the full spread of plant and equipment a construction site demands. From compact skid steers for tight access work through to graders and wheel loaders for large civil projects, the fleet is broad enough that most jobs can be sorted with a single call. A large range of attachments for excavators and skid steers means you can configure machines for specific tasks on site rather than hiring multiple units.

RAM Equipment is ISO certified and a member of the SA Product and Service Directory. For contractors who need to purchase rather than hire, a used equipment inventory is also available alongside the hire fleet.
