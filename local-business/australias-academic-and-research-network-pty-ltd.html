AARNet Australia's Research Network

Australia's National Research & Education Network

Fast, reliable connectivity for research and education

AARNet is Australia's national research and education network — delivering advanced internet connectivity, cyber security, data services, and collaboration tools to the Australian research and education community.

AARNet operates Australia's national research and education network, providing the high-performance connectivity and services that universities, research institutions, and schools need to do their best work. The network is purpose-built for the demands of research — handling large data transfers, enabling real-time collaboration, and supporting cloud-based research platforms.

Beyond raw connectivity, AARNet delivers a suite of services designed specifically for the research and education sector — from cyber security and cloud services through to data management and collaboration tools. These services are designed to support the unique requirements of institutions that generate, store, and share large volumes of research data.

AARNet's work underpins some of Australia's most significant research endeavours. The network enables astronomy at scale, supports international research collaborations, and provides the infrastructure backbone that allows Australian researchers to work with partners around the world.

What AARNet provides

  • Research Network
    Australia's advanced research and education network, providing high-performance internet connectivity purpose-built for the data-intensive needs of universities, research institutions, and schools.
  • Cyber Security
    Security services designed for the research and education sector — protecting institutions and their data while enabling the open collaboration that research depends on.
  • Data Services
    Data management and storage solutions that support the large-scale data requirements of modern research — including tools for data sharing, transfer, and long-term preservation.
  • Collaboration Tools
    Collaboration services that enable researchers, educators, and students to work together effectively — whether on campus, across institutions, or with international partners.
