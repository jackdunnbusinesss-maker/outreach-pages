Fresh & Natural. Simply Raised.

Foster Farms is a California-based farm-to-table poultry producer with a mission to be simply better in all they do. From fresh whole chickens and ready-to-eat meals to foodservice products and an organic range, Foster Farms is your perfect protein.

Foster Farms has been a California institution in poultry production, built on a commitment to farm-to-table quality. The company's mission is straightforward: to be simply better in all they do, from how they raise their birds through to the product that reaches the consumer's table.

The Foster Farms range spans everyday fresh whole chickens through to ready-to-eat meal solutions and a dedicated organic line for consumers looking for Simply Raised options. The company also serves foodservice customers, supplying restaurants, caterers, and institutions with a reliable poultry supply.

Foster Farms products are available through retail grocery channels across the western United States, backed by decades of experience in responsible poultry farming and food manufacturing in California.

The Foster Farms range

  • Fresh Whole Chickens

    Fresh whole chickens raised and processed in California. The foundational Foster Farms product, available through retail grocery partners across the region.

  • Ready-to-Eat Meals

    A range of cooked and prepared chicken products for consumers looking for convenient meal solutions without compromising on quality or provenance.

  • Foodservice Products

    Poultry products formulated for restaurants, caterers, and institutional foodservice operators, providing consistent quality and supply reliability for commercial kitchens.

  • Organic Range

    An organic poultry line for consumers seeking Simply Raised products, produced in line with organic certification standards and the Foster Farms commitment to quality.
