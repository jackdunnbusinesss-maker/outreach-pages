Blackmores has been Australia's most trusted natural health brand for over 90 years — with over 150 products across vitamins, minerals, herbal medicine, fish oil, sports nutrition, and children's health, all manufactured to TGA-licensed pharmaceutical-grade standards. The heritage is a genuine competitive advantage. The problem is that Swisse, backed by H&H Group's marketing budget, is outspending Blackmores on paid media — particularly on Facebook and Instagram — and taking share in the $20–$50 supplement segment.

Blackmores' clinical formulation story and 90-year research heritage are exactly what health-conscious consumers want to hear when they're choosing between Blackmores and a lifestyle brand. The strategy we've built covers how to tell that story at scale — through health education content from Blackmores' naturopaths, paid media by health goal segment, and search capture for the high-intent terms where Blackmores should be winning.

What we'd build for Blackmores

  • Health Goal Segment Campaigns
    Five parallel Facebook/Instagram campaign tracks — Pregnancy, Women's Health, Sports, Kids, General Wellness — each with audience-specific creative targeting the exact consumer considering that health goal. Not one generic vitamin campaign.
  • Health Education Content Engine
    Blackmores naturopath video series: "What form of magnesium actually works?", "How much vitamin D do Australians need?", "Fish oil — what dose matters?" — educational content that positions Blackmores as the clinical authority, not just another supplement brand.
  • Search Defence
    Google Search capture for "pregnancy vitamins Australia", "best fish oil Australia", "magnesium supplement" and "Blackmores vs Swisse" comparison terms — ensuring Blackmores owns the search moment before consumers default to whichever brand appears first.
  • Subscription and Repeat Purchase
    Retargeting and subscription push for Blackmores' highest repeat-purchase products — fish oil, vitamin D, multivitamins — converting one-off buyers into the consistent daily programme customers who generate long-term LTV.
