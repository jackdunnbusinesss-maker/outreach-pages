About us

Termite And Pest Protection is a Brisbane-based pest control company specialising in termite management and pre-construction treatments for builders and the construction industry across the greater Brisbane area.

From comprehensive termite inspections and eradication to full residential pest management covering ants, spiders, rodents, cockroaches, mosquitos, flies, and ticks, we handle every pest challenge with targeted, effective solutions.

Our pre-construction work means we understand the timelines and compliance requirements builders face. We show up on schedule, complete the treatment correctly the first time, and provide the documentation you need for your records.
