Phonenomena was purpose-built as an alternative to going direct with Telstra. As an accredited Enterprise Partner, the team carries full access to the Telstra enterprise network while layering on managed services, software tools, and dedicated support structures designed to work at scale.

The result is a single relationship that covers everything from day-one device rollout through to ongoing repairs, software management, billing oversight, and IoT connectivity.
