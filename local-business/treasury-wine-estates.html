Treasury Wine Estates manages one of the world's most recognised wine portfolios — Penfolds, Wolf Blass, Wynns Coonawarra, Beringer, and the recently acquired DAOU Vineyards. The brand depth is there. The premium positioning is established. The gap is in how that brand depth is captured and converted in a digital landscape where wine buying decisions are increasingly made before anyone walks into a bottle shop.

Premium wine consumers research before they buy. They're reading about vintages, comparing labels, watching harvest videos, and looking at wine education content — and the brands that show up in those moments are the ones that end up in the basket. The strategy we've built for TWE covers paid media across the Gartner recognition moments, content that captures the premium research audience, and a competitive response to Pernod Ricard and Constellation that doesn't cede the digital conversation.
