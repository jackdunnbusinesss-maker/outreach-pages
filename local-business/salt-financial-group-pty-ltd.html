About the Firm

A diversified practice built on expertise and trust

Salt Financial Group is a Melbourne-based financial services firm located in Camberwell, VIC. The firm brings together specialists across accounting, wealth management, financial planning, personal insurance, and retirement planning — so clients work with a coordinated team rather than a collection of separate advisers.

Many people believe they can't afford a financial adviser until they already have wealth. The Salt philosophy is the opposite: the earlier you start working with the right team, the faster your financial outcomes change. Clients consistently find that working with Salt is straightforward to begin, and that results follow quickly from following a structured plan.
