About Montara Wealth

Based in the heart of Sydney's CBD at 309–315 George Street, Level 6, Montara Wealth is a team of passionate financial professionals dedicated to helping Australians take genuine control of their financial future.

We specialise in working with people in their 30s, 40s and 50s — those who are earning well but aren't yet seeing their efforts translate into real wealth. Whether you're juggling a mortgage and kids, planning your first investment, or trying to see a retirement date on the horizon, our advisers take the pressure off.

Our approach is holistic and strategic. We build bespoke financial plans that reflect your unique goals, challenges, and lifestyle — with a deep understanding of the current financial landscape at every step. At Montara Wealth, clarity and confidence are built into every engagement.
