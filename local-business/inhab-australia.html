About InHab Australia

InHab Australia is a NDIS registered provider of Specialist Disability Accommodation (SDA) and Support Coordination services. Our work sits at the intersection of disability support and the property sector — two worlds that must work together to create housing that is genuinely fit for purpose.

We help participants with SDA funding in their plan to choose their location and define what is required in a home to live their best lives. We help developers and builders design and build the required accommodation that meets the needs of people with disability who become long-term, stable, and satisfied tenants.

Our Vision is "To be the leading SDA Provider of choice where people with a disability can find high quality, fit-for-purpose accommodation solutions and establish a home as a base for life." Our Mission is to bring together people with a disability and the property sector to deliver leading, high-quality SDA — creating long-term homes and sustainable investments.

What we do

  • SDA Navigation — Participants

    We empower NDIS participants with SDA funding to choose their location and define exactly what their home needs to be — helping them navigate a complex system from start to finish.

  • Design & Development Support — Developers

    We enable developers and builders to design and build the required accommodation that meets the needs of people with disability — creating long-term, stable tenancy outcomes and sustainable investment returns.

  • Specialist Support Coordination

    As an NDIS registered Support Coordination provider, we help participants understand and implement their NDIS plan — connecting the right services, the right housing, and the right outcomes.

  • End-to-End SDA Matching

    From registering interest through to placement and ongoing support, InHab manages the full SDA pathway — bringing all parties together to create successful outcomes for everyone involved.

How the SDA pathway works

1
Register your interest
Call 1800 464 222 or complete the interest registration form. The InHab team will make contact and begin understanding your situation.
2
Review your NDIS plan
InHab works with you to understand your SDA funding, eligibility, and what your plan allows for in terms of accommodation requirements.
3
Define your home
You choose your preferred location and define what is required in a home to live your best life. This plan drives the entire process.
4
Move into long-term housing
InHab brings together participants, property developers, and support services to deliver a fit-for-purpose home — a base for life.
