Real estate done on the right principles

Arthur Conias Real Estate has been serving Brisbane's inner west since 1972 — built on a straightforward commitment to ethics, values, and client care that the industry doesn't always deliver on.

We operate two offices in Brisbane's inner west — Toowong and Ashgrove — with a dedicated property management team handling everything from appraisals to ongoing tenancy management. Whether you're selling, buying, or need a property managed, you deal with people who know this market.

We're Jenman Approved — a consumer protection standard that holds agencies to a higher level of honesty and transparency than the industry norm. It's not a badge we put up lightly, and it shapes how every transaction is handled.

If you're thinking about what your property is worth, what tenants are paying in your area, or simply whether now is the right time to move — reach out. The appraisals are free and there's no pressure attached.
