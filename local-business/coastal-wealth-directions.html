About the practice

Financial planning that fits real lives

Everyone's experience with money is different. Our work starts by understanding yours — then building a plan that actually fits it.

Coastal Wealth Directions is the culmination of more than 25 years of experience in the financial services industry. It was created to offer a financial planning service that is both comprehensive and completely tailored.

Our clients are diverse — across ages, stages of life, and the resources they bring with them. So our strategies are diverse too. We meet you where you are now, and help guide you toward where you want to go.

Your lifestyle and financial goals are personal and important to you. We help you build some substance beneath them, with an actionable plan designed to give you real choices at every stage.

Tania Powick
Owner & Principal Financial Planner
