True wealth is about living fully, not just the numbers

Truss Wealth Advisors helps retirees, business owners and dentists in the Indianapolis area build and protect financial lives that reflect what actually matters to them.

Since 2003, Truss Wealth Advisors has worked with over 1,500 families across the Indianapolis area. Our team carries more than 100 years of collective experience in wealth management, financial planning and retirement strategy. We are part of the Northwestern Mutual network, giving our clients access to a deep bench of institutional resources alongside the personal attention of a local practice.

We give back. Ten percent of the fees we earn go to charitable causes. That commitment reflects what we believe wealth is for: not accumulation as an end in itself, but the freedom to live well and contribute to something larger.

Our clients come to us at pivotal moments, planning for retirement, selling a business, navigating a major life transition or thinking about what they want to leave behind. We work through those moments with clarity, patience and a genuine interest in their lives beyond the financial plan.

How we work with you

  • Wealth Management and Financial Planning

    Comprehensive wealth management and financial planning built around your specific goals and timeline. We cover investment strategy, cash flow, insurance and estate considerations in an integrated plan.

  • Retirement Planning

    Retirement planning that goes beyond account balances. We help you understand what your retirement actually costs, when you can realistically retire and how to make your assets last.

  • Tax-Efficient Strategies

    Investment and distribution strategies designed to reduce the tax drag on your wealth over time. We coordinate with your CPA to make sure your financial plan and tax plan work together.

  • Business Succession Planning

    Planning for business owners who need to think about what comes after. Whether you are planning a sale, a family transfer or a management buyout, we help you structure it to protect what you have built.

  • Legacy Planning

    Strategies for passing wealth to the next generation or to charitable causes in a way that reflects your values and minimizes unnecessary costs and complexity.
