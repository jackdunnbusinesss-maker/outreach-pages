Our Schemes & Products

Superannuation solutions built around government service

PSSap

Public Sector Superannuation accumulation plan — a flexible, low-cost super fund for current Australian Government employees building their retirement savings.

PSS

Public Sector Superannuation Scheme — a defined benefit fund for eligible Australian Government employees with long-term career commitments to the public sector.

CSS

Commonwealth Superannuation Scheme — Australia's original public sector super scheme, supporting members who have served the government for decades.

MilitarySuper & DFRDB

Tailored retirement schemes for ADF members — MilitarySuper for accumulation-phase members and the Defence Force Retirement and Death Benefits scheme for long-serving personnel.

CSCri — Retirement Income Stream

Turn your super savings into a regular, tax-effective income in retirement. CSCri is designed to support your financial wellbeing through every year of your post-work life.

ADF Cover & lifePLUS Insurance

ADF Cover and ADF lifePLUS Protect provide death, total and permanent disability, and income protection cover purpose-built for the unique risks of ADF service.

About CSC

Dedicated to those who serve Australia

Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation exists for one purpose: to help current and former Australian Government employees and ADF members make better retirement decisions. We don't serve the general public — we serve the people who serve the nation.

That focus means every product, every piece of guidance, and every specialist on our team understands the unique career paths, entitlements, and schemes that apply to government and defence service. Whether you're early in your career with PSSap, approaching retirement with CSS, or transitioning out of the ADF, CSC has the expertise to support you at every stage.

Our goal is straightforward: give you the information you need — according to your age and life stage — to build the retirement you deserve.
