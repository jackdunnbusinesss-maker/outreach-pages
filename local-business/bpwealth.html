Our heritage

Over two decades of
WA financial planning

Blueprint Wealth has been advising Perth and Western Australian families since 2001. We have guided clients through two property cycles, multiple market corrections, compulsory super changes, and the specific challenges facing WA's resource-sector workforce.

Our advisors understand the financial realities of FIFO rosters, mining-sector salaries, irregular income patterns, and the window many WA workers have to build significant super balances — and how to make the most of it before contribution caps tighten.

How we work

A structured process, not a one-off transaction

We follow a clear four-step engagement model — so you always know where you are and what comes next.

Step 01
Discovery
We map your current position — assets, super balances, income, goals, and time horizon — before any advice is given.
Step 02
Strategy
We model multiple scenarios and present a statement of advice tailored to your situation, not a templated plan.
Step 03
Implementation
We act on the agreed strategy — restructuring super, establishing an SMSF, rebalancing portfolios, or arranging estate planning documents.
Step 04
Ongoing review
Legislation changes. Markets move. Life changes. We review your plan annually and adjust as needed throughout our relationship.
Client case studies

Real strategies, real outcomes

Three documented examples of how Blueprint Wealth has helped WA clients structure their financial affairs to achieve specific goals.

SMSF · Property
Buying Property Inside an SMSF
A client wanted to hold an investment property within their self-managed super fund using a limited recourse borrowing arrangement. We structured the LRBA, handled trustee obligations, and coordinated with their lender to complete settlement inside the fund.
Outcome: Property acquired in compliant SMSF structure, rental income taxed at 15%

Estate Planning with Tax Benefits
A client approaching retirement sought to transfer wealth to adult children in a tax-efficient way. We structured a binding death benefit nomination and testamentary trust arrangement to minimise estate tax exposure on the super balance.
Outcome: Significant reduction in estimated tax liability on estate transfer
Read case study → SMSF · Tax Savings
Tax Savings Through SMSF
A WA mining professional on a high salary used concessional contributions and an SMSF structure to reduce personal income tax while accelerating super growth. We modelled contribution strategies within the $30,000 concessional cap.
Outcome: Measurable annual tax savings and faster super accumulation
