The Australian property investment industry has a problem: too many advisors operate without meaningful qualifications, without independent oversight, and with undisclosed conflicts of interest. ASPIRE Property Advisor Network was built to change that.

Our mission is to disrupt an unregulated industry by setting and enforcing a genuine standard — one that puts investor outcomes ahead of commissions. Every advisor in the ASPIRE network holds a Real Estate Licence, maintains Qualified Property Investment Advisor (QPIA) accreditation through PIPA, carries professional indemnity insurance, and discloses all fees upfront.

The result is a network investors can trust, and a platform serious advisors want to be part of.

What ASPIRE accreditation requires

  • Qualified

    Every ASPIRE advisor holds a Qualified Property Investment Advisor (QPIA) designation through the Property Investment Professionals of Australia (PIPA) — the industry's recognised professional standard.

  • Licensed

    All advisors carry a current Real Estate Licence and are professionally engaged by investors — not developers, project marketers, or any other party with a financial interest in the sale.

  • Independent

    ASPIRE advisors operate with no conflict of interest. Fee disclosure is mandatory. Investors know exactly what they're paying and to whom, before any advice is given.

  • Insured

    Professional indemnity insurance is a non-negotiable requirement for accreditation. Investors are protected. Advisors are accountable.

  • Accountable

    All network members hold active membership with PIPA and adhere to ASPIRE's code of conduct — a set of ethical obligations that govern how advisors operate with every client.
