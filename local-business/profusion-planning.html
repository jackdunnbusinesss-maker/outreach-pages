About Profusion Planning

At Profusion Planning, we're committed to building trusting, long-term relationships with our clients. As life changes, you need a plan flexible enough to change with it.

We are a dedicated, privately owned WA financial planning business that holds our own Australian Financial Services Licence. That independence means we put our clients' interests at the centre of every recommendation — not the interests of a large institution or product provider.

We specialise in holistic advice and operate on a flat fee schedule — giving you 100% transparency at every step of the process. No hidden charges. No commissions. No conflicts.

What we do

  • Personalised Approach

    Every client is different. We take the time to understand your full financial picture before making a single recommendation — building a strategy that actually fits your life.

  • Investment Philosophy

    Clear, conservative investment strategies that are easy to understand and built for the long term. No jargon, no unnecessary complexity — just focused on enabling informed decision making.

  • Goal Based Advice

    We anchor every plan to what matters most to you — whether that's buying a home, funding retirement, or building long-term wealth. Your goals drive the strategy.

  • Family & Estate Planning

    Comprehensive planning that protects your family's financial future — structuring your estate so your wealth goes where you intend it to.

  • Superannuation & GESB

    Specialist expertise in GESB accounts, untaxed funds, and defined benefit schemes — areas that are widely misunderstood but play a major role in WA retirement outcomes.
