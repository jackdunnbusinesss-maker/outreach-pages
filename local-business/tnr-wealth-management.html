About TNR Wealth Management

The TNR Wealth team are wealth planners and specialists in personal financial planning — ready to assist you and your family in creating wealth for a financially secure future. Based across Byron Bay and Lismore, we serve the Northern Rivers community with dedicated, expert advice.

Our financial advisers understand that every client's financial and life circumstances are different. We customise our approach to suit your needs, building a strong and trustworthy relationship along the way. We're committed to excellence in every aspect of our work.

TNR Wealth advisers are authorised representatives of Fortnum Private Wealth (AFSL 357306), providing you with the security and professional standards that come with a fully licensed advisory practice.

Our services

  • Retirement Planning

    Strategic guidance to prepare you for retirement — whether you're years away or approaching it now. We build a plan that matches your lifestyle goals and income requirements.

  • Superannuation Advice

  • Wealth Creation & Investment

    A structured approach to growing your wealth over time — investment strategy, portfolio construction, and ongoing advice as your circumstances evolve.
