Our approach

How we can help you

Forbes Wealth Planning works with Adelaide families, pre-retirees, and professionals who want a planner who actually knows their whole picture — not just their super balance.

1

Initial consultation

A focused $275 session to understand your situation, your goals, and whether we're the right fit. Refundable when you proceed with a full advice engagement.

2

Personalised strategy

We build a comprehensive Statement of Advice that covers every area relevant to you — health, protection, retirement, and investment — in one coordinated plan.

3

Implementation & review

We handle the setup, then check in regularly as your life changes. The plan adapts with you, not against you.

4

Ongoing partnership

"We share a strong sense of mutual commitment that creates a synergy our clients can feel." Long-term relationships, not one-off transactions.
