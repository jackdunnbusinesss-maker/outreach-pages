Shine Lawyers is a national Australian law firm focused on personal injury and compensation claims. The team assists clients through insurance claims processes, including superannuation-based claims, with dedicated client service available seven days a week.

Shine Lawyers operates nationally across Australia, focusing on personal injury and compensation law. The firm assists individuals navigating insurance and superannuation claims that arise from injury, illness, or death, including claims held within industry and retail superannuation funds.

The firm's approach is centred on the client. The Client Service Team is available seven days a week, 7am to 7pm Monday to Friday, providing access to legal support outside standard business hours for people dealing with difficult circumstances.

Shine Lawyers acts for claimants across a range of insurance claim types, helping them understand their entitlements and manage the claims process with the assistance of legal representation.

Claims Shine Lawyers assists with

  • Total Permanent Disability (TPD)

    Assistance with TPD insurance claims, which may be held inside superannuation funds. Shine Lawyers helps clients understand whether they may be eligible to make a claim and manages the claims process on their behalf.

  • Income Protection (IP)

    Support for clients making Income Protection insurance claims, including claims held through superannuation. The team assists with preparing and lodging claims and managing insurer communications.

  • Death Benefit Claims

    Assistance for beneficiaries making death benefit claims through superannuation funds or life insurance policies, including navigating trustee decision-making processes.

  • Trauma Insurance Claims

    Support for individuals making trauma insurance claims following a diagnosis of a serious medical condition, helping clients understand their policy entitlements and manage the claims process.
