Cition — Cognitive Cities

The Next Generation of Urban Living Starts in Melbourne

Cition Pty Ltd — the development subsidiary of Investors Direct Financial Group — is building Cognitive Cities that combine collaborative education campuses, clean-energy innovation precincts, and liveable 20-minute residential neighbourhoods. The Australian Education City at East Werribee is the first proof of concept.

1
Learning City

Collaborative campuses hosting universities, TAFEs, and K–12 schools — offering lifelong and remote learning for 20,000+ domestic and international students.

2
Innovation District

R&D and industry partnerships centred on clean energy and digital connectivity. Modelled on proven precincts like Seattle South Lake Union and Boston's Innovation District.

3
Residential Community

World-class housing, student accommodation, aged care, and social housing — all within a 20-minute neighbourhood designed to eliminate long commutes and build genuine community.
