At Horizon Wealth Advisory, we work with Queenslanders at every stage of life — from young professionals just starting to build wealth, to families navigating estate planning, to business owners planning for retirement.

We're a boutique firm, which means you deal directly with an experienced adviser who knows your situation — not a rotating team or a call centre. Our office is based at 6/123 Eagle St, Brisbane City, and we service clients across the greater Brisbane region and beyond.

What we help you with

Retirement Planning

Whether retirement is five years or twenty years away, we help you map out exactly what you need and build a strategy that gets you there — with the flexibility to adapt as life changes.

Investments & Wealth Creation

From investment portfolios to wealth creation strategies, we help you put your money to work in a way that aligns with your risk tolerance, timeframe, and goals.

Cash Flow Management

We help you get a clear picture of your finances, eliminate inefficiencies, and build habits that create long-term financial momentum — not just short-term fixes.

Personal Insurance & Income Protection

Your income is your most valuable asset. We structure insurance solutions — including income protection and life cover — to make sure you and your family are protected if the unexpected happens.

SMSF Advice

Self-managed super funds offer control and flexibility — but they come with real compliance obligations. We guide you through setup, strategy, and ongoing management so your SMSF works for you.

Estate Planning & Binding Nominations

Making sure your assets go where you intend — and that your family is protected — requires more than a will. We walk you through binding nominations and estate structures that hold up when it matters.

Business & Financial Planning

For business owners, personal and business finances are rarely separate. We help you align both — from protecting what you've built to planning your eventual exit.

Financial Planning for Young Professionals

Starting early is the single biggest advantage in wealth building. We work with young professionals who want to get ahead — on super, savings, insurance, and long-term strategy.
