Financing infrastructure where others hesitate to lead

National Standard Finance is a U.S.-based investment firm that structures and arranges financing for essential infrastructure and economic development initiatives around the world.

National Standard Finance works on the transactions that require more than a standard financing template. Sovereign-linked project finance, public-private partnerships, long-lifecycle infrastructure assets and essential services all carry structural complexity that calls for a firm with deep experience and institutional discipline. That is what we bring to every mandate.

Our focus is on strong credit fundamentals and long-term project lifecycles. We do not chase volume. We take the time to understand each transaction, its counterparties and the environment it sits within, then structure financing that reflects the actual risk profile rather than a generic approximation of it.

With a U.S. base and a genuinely global footprint, we work with governments, development institutions, infrastructure funds and corporate sponsors across multiple jurisdictions. The transactions we take on are institutional in scale and significance, and we treat them accordingly.

What we do

  • Strategic Infrastructure Financing

    Financing for essential infrastructure and economic development projects, including energy, transport, water and public services. We work across the full transaction lifecycle from structuring through to financial close.

  • Structured Debt

    Institutional-grade structured debt solutions for complex infrastructure transactions. We design debt structures that match project cash flows and credit characteristics, not off-the-shelf templates.

  • PPP Advisory

    Public-private partnership advisory for government and institutional clients. We help structure arrangements that allocate risk appropriately and deliver bankable outcomes for all parties.

  • Sovereign and Government-Linked Financing

    Project finance for sovereign and government-linked sponsors. We understand the considerations that come with public-sector clients and structure accordingly, maintaining institutional discipline throughout.
