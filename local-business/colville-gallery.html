Gallery services

  • Fine Art Exhibitions

    A rotating programme of contemporary Australian art exhibitions presented at 30 Collins Street in central Hobart. Each show is curated to bring ambitious work to audiences across Tasmania and beyond.

  • COLLECT Art Purchase Scheme

    COLLECT is our art purchase scheme designed to make collecting original contemporary works more accessible. It allows buyers to acquire quality work and spread the cost over time, without compromising on provenance or artist relationships.

  • Art Curation and Advisory

    Director Trudi Curtis provides curation and advisory services for collectors at every level. Whether you are building a first collection or acquiring a significant work, we offer guidance grounded in art historical knowledge and curatorial experience.

About the gallery

Colville Gallery is a contemporary art gallery at 30 Collins Street in the heart of Hobart, Tasmania. The gallery is directed by Trudi Curtis, an Art Historian and Curator with a deep commitment to contemporary practice and to the artists she represents.

The gallery operates as a dedicated space for living artists working across a broad range of disciplines. Every exhibition is developed in close collaboration with the artist, with attention to how each work is shown and received.

The COLLECT Art Purchase Scheme reflects our belief that collecting original art should be within reach for more people. We work with buyers at all stages to find the right work and the right arrangement.

"A contemporary art gallery in central Hobart, led by an Art Historian and Curator."
