What We Offer

Gallery Services

Exhibitions

A rotating programme of contemporary art exhibitions presented in the heart of Hobart. Each show is curated to bring ambitious, thought-provoking work to audiences across Tasmania and beyond.

Art Sales

Works presented in the gallery are available for acquisition. Our team can guide collectors at every level — whether you are purchasing your first piece or adding to an established collection.

COLLECT Art Purchase Scheme

COLLECT is an art purchase scheme that makes acquiring original contemporary works more accessible. It is designed for buyers who wish to build a collection gradually, spreading the cost of a purchase over time without sacrificing quality or provenance.

About the Gallery

Led by expertise.
Rooted in Hobart.

Colville Gallery is directed by Trudi Curtis, an Art Historian and Curator with a deep commitment to contemporary practice. Located at 30 Collins Street in central Hobart, the gallery operates as a dedicated space for living artists working across a broad range of disciplines.

"A contemporary art gallery in central Hobart, led by an Art Historian and Curator."

Director — Trudi Curtis, Art Historian & Curator
