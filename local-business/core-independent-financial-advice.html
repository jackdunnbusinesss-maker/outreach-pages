Why this matters

Most "financial advisers" are paid by the products they recommend.

Under Australian law, only a handful of firms meet the bar to call themselves genuinely independent. We're one of them. No asset-based commissions. No kickbacks. No backstop from a big institution steering the advice.

What that means for you: the advice you get is the advice that's best for you, not the advice that pays best. Simple as that.
