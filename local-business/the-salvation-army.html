5-minute strategy walkthrough — donor acquisition and cause communications

The Salvation Army has operated in the UK since 1865 — over 150 years of consistent service to the most vulnerable people in Britain. Today, The Salvation Army operates 100+ emergency shelters and supported housing services, residential addiction rehabilitation centres across the UK, and is the UK government's prime contractor for modern slavery victim support under the National Referral Mechanism. The depth of the operation is extraordinary. The awareness of that depth among the UK public — beyond Christmas kettles and charity shops — is not.

The Salvation Army's year-round impact — beds tonight, meals today, addiction rehabilitation, modern slavery support — is the story that converts donors who care about direct, immediate human impact. The Christmas appeal remains the highest-value fundraising window, but the donors who give most over a lifetime are those who understand the year-round operation. The strategy we've built for The Salvation Army covers three simultaneous goals: individual donor acquisition, regular giving conversion, and volunteer recruitment — across all three primary causes and the Christmas appeal.

What we'd build for The Salvation Army

  • Cause-Specific Donor Acquisition
    Facebook campaigns across three cause tracks — homelessness (shelter and meals), addiction rehabilitation (accessible programme messaging), and modern slavery support (government-commissioned role) — each with specific impact metrics and donation CTAs. Not one generic "give to charity" ad.
  • Regular Giving Conversion
    Facebook and email retargeting of one-off donors with the regular giving case: "£5/month vs a one-off donation — here's why it matters for the people we serve." Regular giving is the highest-LTV donor conversion and most charities don't pursue it hard enough.
  • Christmas Appeal
    Full-channel push from October through December — toy appeals, Christmas dinners, shelter funding, gifts for children in crisis families. Google Search for "Christmas charity UK", Facebook visual content, corporate match giving on LinkedIn. The highest-ROI fundraising window of the year, treated as a campaign, not just a brand moment.
  • Volunteer Recruitment
    Facebook and Instagram targeting UK adults 22–45 with specific volunteer roles: "2 hours Saturday morning at your local food bank", "help sort donations at the charity shop", "befriending visit for an isolated older person" — concrete, time-specific, immediately actionable.
