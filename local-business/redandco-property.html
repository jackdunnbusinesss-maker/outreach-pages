About Red & Co

Red & Co is an integrated property group operating across development, property management, and investment. The team combines hands-on development experience with professional rental management — giving landlords and investors a single point of contact for every stage of a property's lifecycle.

From current build-to-rent projects through to completed developments, Red & Co's track record reflects a commitment to quality construction, active asset management, and tenant outcomes. The group also runs Red & Co Funds, providing investors access to curated property opportunities.

What we do

  • Property Management

    Professional rental management covering tenant placement, maintenance coordination, and ongoing landlord reporting — so owners stay informed without the day-to-day noise.

  • Rental Services

    A dedicated rental division focused on matching quality tenants with well-maintained properties, backed by processes that protect both landlord returns and tenant experience.

  • Residential Development

    Residential projects from site acquisition through to completion — with current, completed, and build-to-rent developments delivered to market by a team with genuine development expertise.

  • Build to Rent

    Purpose-built rental communities managed in-house, combining development discipline with long-term asset management to deliver consistent tenancy outcomes.

  • Red & Co Funds

    Investment fund structures giving qualified investors access to the pipeline and asset base that Red & Co develops and manages directly.
