Our Approach

We start by getting to know you.

Before we ever talk numbers, we ask three simple questions. Because the best retirement plan isn't built from spreadsheets alone — it's built around what actually matters to you.

At Aurora Wealth, lifestyle-focused planning means your retirement strategy starts with who you are, not just what you have. Micheal takes the time to understand your financial situation and personal goals before mapping out a strategy — whether that's meeting at our Narre Warren office, at your place, or over Zoom.

There's no jargon, no pressure, and no one-size-fits-all solutions. Just honest, independent advice built around the retirement you actually want to live.

The first step is a free discovery call — a relaxed 20-minute conversation to see if we're the right fit for you.

Start the Conversation
Who are you?
We want to understand your life, your situation, and what brings you to thinking about retirement now. Your answers shape everything that follows.
What do you enjoy doing?
Travel? Time with grandkids? Weekend camping? A hobby you've never had enough hours for? Your passions drive your retirement income targets — and your timeline.
What's a personal goal of your own?
Not a financial goal — a personal one. The trip you've always wanted, the milestone you're working towards, the version of retirement that excites you. That's our north star.

What We Do

Specialist retirement advice,
nothing else.

We don't try to be everything to everyone. Aurora Wealth specialises in one thing — helping pre-retirees and retirees in Australia build confident, funded futures.

🌅
Retirement Planning & Strategy
Personalised retirement income strategies built around your lifestyle goals, not a template. We map your income needs, expected expenses, and the drawdown approach that lets you retire with confidence.
💼
Superannuation Advice
Consolidation, fee audits, concessional contributions, preservation age planning, and transfer balance cap guidance. We help you squeeze every dollar from your super before and during retirement.
📋
Personal Financial Planning
From debt reduction and tax-efficient investing to salary sacrificing and budgeting — a holistic financial plan that brings your whole picture into focus as retirement approaches.
🏛️
Centrelink & Age Pension
Navigating Centrelink eligibility, the assets test, and the income test can feel overwhelming. We help you structure your affairs to maximise your entitlements without leaving money on the table.
🛡️
Insurance & Protection Review
Life, TPD, trauma, and income protection — reviewed against your actual needs as you approach retirement. No unnecessary cover, no gaps that could derail your plans.
📜
Estate Planning Guidance
Beneficiary nominations, binding death nominations, and structuring assets to pass to the people you love — efficiently and with minimal friction for your family.
