As Adelaide grows outward, Weeks & Macklin grows with it. The team builds deep expertise across the full metropolitan area — eastern, western, southern, and northern suburbs — while remaining rooted in the community they started in.

2025 — 50 Years On
Half a century. Still family-run.
Fifty years later, Max and Jemma de Meyrick lead an experienced team still guided by the same values: family, relationships, and integrity. Significant repeat and referral business speaks to what that consistency delivers over time.

Most real estate agencies in Adelaide have come and gone over the past half-century. Weeks & Macklin is still here — and still doing things the same way.

"An inherent passion for the local area, strong ties to our community, and an intimate understanding of our market."

That's not marketing language. It's a fair description of what 50 years of operating in one place looks like. The team at Weeks & Macklin have watched suburbs develop, seen markets cycle, and built long-running relationships with clients who come back — and refer their families and friends.

Proudly South Australian owned and operated, the business has never chased growth for its own sake. The focus has always been residential sales, residential property management, and land subdivision consulting — done well, for people who live and invest in this city.

In an industry that can feel transactional, that approach has proven its worth over and over again.
