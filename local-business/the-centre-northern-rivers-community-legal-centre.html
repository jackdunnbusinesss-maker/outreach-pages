Free Legal Services — Northern Rivers NSW

A just and safe society for all

The Northern Rivers Community Legal Centre provides free legal advice, representation, and support to people across the Northern Rivers region. Whoever you are, whatever your situation, you have the right to understand your legal options.

Legal problems don't sort themselves out, and the cost of getting advice shouldn't stand in the way of finding a solution. At the Northern Rivers Community Legal Centre, all services are free of charge for people in the Northern Rivers community. The team handles a wide range of legal matters, from tenancy disputes and family law through to domestic and family violence support and disaster recovery assistance.

The Centre also provides dedicated Aboriginal Services, recognising the particular needs and experiences of First Nations community members. Staff acknowledge the Bundjalung Nation as the traditional custodians of this land. Everyone who comes through is treated with respect and without judgement.

The office is located at 56 Carrington Street, Lismore, and is open Monday to Thursday from 9am to 4pm. Phone lines remain open on Fridays for anyone who needs to get in touch. The freecall number 1800 689 889 is available for those who cannot afford a local call.

How we can help you

  • Legal Services
    Free legal advice and representation across a broad range of civil legal matters. Whether you need someone to explain your rights or support you through a legal process, the Centre is here for you.
  • Tenant Services
    Tenants' advice and advocacy to help renters understand their rights and resolve disputes with landlords. This includes support with repairs, bonds, evictions, and rental agreements.
  • Family Law & Domestic Violence Support
    Sensitive, confidential support for people navigating family law matters or experiencing domestic and family violence. The Centre works with individuals to access the legal protections available to them.
  • Aboriginal Services
    Culturally appropriate legal services for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander community members, delivered with an understanding of the unique legal and social contexts they may face.
  • Disaster Response and Recovery
    Legal assistance for community members dealing with the aftermath of natural disasters, including insurance disputes, housing issues, and government assistance matters.

Need legal help? We're here for you.
