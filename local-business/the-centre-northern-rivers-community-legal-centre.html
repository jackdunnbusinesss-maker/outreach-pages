About Us

Here for Everyone in the Northern Rivers

The Northern Rivers Community Legal Centre provides free, high-quality legal services to people across the Northern Rivers of NSW. We are here to help with tenancy, domestic violence, disaster recovery, Aboriginal services, and family law.

Our services are free and confidential. We work with people who may not otherwise be able to access legal help, ensuring that justice is not determined by your ability to pay.

We acknowledge the people of the Bundjalung Nation, Traditional Custodians of the land on which we work and pay our respects to Elders past and present.
