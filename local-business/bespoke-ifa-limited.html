Independent advice.
Tailored to your financial life.

Bespoke IFA has provided truly independent financial advice to high earners, those approaching retirement, and families passing down wealth since 1995. FCA regulated. Top Rated Firm. 96% of clients would recommend.

Independent advice means something specific. It means Bespoke IFA is not tied to any product provider, platform, or investment house. The recommendations you receive are based on your situation alone, drawn from the full range of available options in the market.

Since 1995, the firm has operated from Crowthorne, Surrey, working with clients whose financial situations have grown complex enough to warrant genuine expertise: high earners with pension and tax considerations that require careful planning, individuals in the final years before retirement who need a clear transition plan, and families thinking seriously about how wealth passes to the next generation.

The firm's five-step process is designed to make advice feel less like a transaction and more like a collaborative conversation. It starts with an initial call, moves into a discovery meeting where the full picture is understood, then produces clear recommendations, implementation, and annual reviews that keep the plan current. Ninety-nine per cent of clients rate the response speed as satisfactory or better. The advice is thorough and the communication is prompt.

Who Bespoke IFA works with

  • High Earners
    Financial planning for professionals and executives whose income creates complex pension, tax, and investment decisions that benefit from structured, independent guidance.
  • Approaching Retirement
    Retirement planning for those in the final years of work, covering income drawdown, pension consolidation, tax efficiency, and building a retirement that funds the life you have planned for.
  • Passing Down Wealth
    Estate and inheritance planning strategies that help families transfer wealth to the next generation in a tax-efficient and deliberate way, with structures that reflect your intentions.

The five-step process

  • Step 1 — Initial Call
    A straightforward conversation to understand your situation, what you are trying to achieve, and whether Bespoke IFA is the right fit for your needs.
  • Step 2 — Discovery Meeting
    A thorough review of your full financial picture: assets, liabilities, income, goals, risk tolerance, and anything else that shapes a sound plan.
  • Step 3 — Recommendations
    Clear, independent recommendations drawn from the full range of market options, structured around your specific circumstances and objectives.
  • Step 4 — Implementation
    The agreed plan is put in place with precision, with every detail handled so that nothing is left undone or misaligned with the strategy.
  • Step 5 — Annual Reviews
    Regular reviews to ensure the plan reflects your current situation, adjusting for life changes, market conditions, and evolving financial goals.
