Your Home Hunter is Australia's premier renters advocacy service. Since 2018, we have helped individuals, families, and corporate clients find and secure the right rental property, including properties that never hit the public listings.

The rental market is genuinely exhausting. Weekend inspections, applications that disappear without acknowledgement, properties gone before you have had a chance to arrange a viewing. Most renters go through this process completely alone, competing against dozens of other applicants with no real advantage and no one advocating for them.

Your Home Hunter changes that. As a renters advocate, we work exclusively for you, not the agent, not the landlord. We search both on-market and off-market properties, handle the application process, and negotiate terms on your behalf. The goal is straightforward: find you the right property and help you secure it.

Whether you need full-service support from the search through to moving in, a professional property review before you commit, or just an hour of honest advice, Your Home Hunter has a service that fits. Serving renters across Melbourne, Brisbane, and Adelaide since 2018.

How we help renters

  • Full Service Package

    We handle everything. From identifying properties that match your brief (including off-market options) through to inspections, application strategy, and negotiating your lease terms. You focus on your move, not the hunt.

  • Property Review & Inspect

    Found a property you like but can't make the inspection? Or want an independent professional assessment before you commit? We attend on your behalf, report back honestly, and help you decide whether to proceed.

  • Consulting Service

    A focused session with one of our advocates to work through your specific situation. Useful if you are struggling to get applications approved, unsure how to position yourself, or navigating a tricky rental circumstance.

  • DIY Guide

    For renters who want to handle the process themselves but with a real advantage. Our guide covers how to search effectively, write competitive applications, and approach negotiations, drawn from years of working the market from both sides.
