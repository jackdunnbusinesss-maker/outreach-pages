About Beyond Housing

Beyond Housing believes that a stable, secure and affordable home creates the foundation for change and opportunity. When housing is safe and consistent, everything else becomes possible — health, education, employment, family stability.

We provide housing support services across the Hume region of Victoria and NSW, working with individuals and families who are experiencing homelessness or at risk of losing their home. Whether you need emergency accommodation tonight or support to maintain your private rental long-term, Beyond Housing is here.

How Beyond Housing Helps

  • Crisis Accommodation

    Immediate, safe accommodation for individuals and families experiencing homelessness — available around the clock through our 24/7 crisis line.

  • Transitional Housing

    Medium-term housing support to bridge the gap between crisis and stability — giving you time and support to find a permanent solution.

  • Long-term Housing Support

    Ongoing support to help you maintain stable housing — including tenancy advice, advocacy, and connection to community services.

  • Private Rental Assistance

    Support to access and sustain private rental housing — including bond assistance, advocacy with landlords, and budget support.

  • Family Violence Support

    Safe housing and wrap-around support for people escaping family violence — with a trauma-informed approach and confidential assistance.

  • Youth Housing (16–24)

    Dedicated housing support for young people aged 16 to 24 — providing stability and connection to services during a critical life stage.

  • After-Hours Urgent Maintenance

    Emergency maintenance support for tenants in Beyond Housing properties — because urgent repairs can't always wait until business hours.
