About Telstra

Connecting Australians — wherever they are

Telstra is Australia's largest telecommunications company — operating the nation's biggest mobile network to keep Australians connected at home, at work, and everywhere in between. From major cities to remote communities, the Telstra network reaches further than any other provider in the country.

Our product range covers every connectivity need: 5G and 4G mobile plans for individuals and families, NBN broadband and Home 5G for household internet, pre-paid mobile for flexible users, and streaming services bundled for entertainment at home. Every plan is backed by our network and supported by our team.

We also understand that circumstances change. Our financial assistance team is available to help customers experiencing hardship — because staying connected shouldn't depend on your circumstances.
