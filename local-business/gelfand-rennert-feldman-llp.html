Trusted financial counsel for leaders in entertainment

Since 1967, Gelfand, Rennert & Feldman has provided a trusted perspective to leaders in music, film, television, sports, and entertainment.

The business of entertainment is not like other industries. Income arrives in irregular waves. Royalties flow through complex multi-party agreements. Tax obligations cross state and international lines. The financial decisions made during peak earning years have consequences that extend decades beyond them. Gelfand, Rennert & Feldman was built specifically for this reality.

Established in 1967, GRF has spent more than five decades serving musicians, actors, athletes, producers, directors and the estates and families that surround them. The firm operates across offices in Los Angeles and New York, placing it at the centre of the industries it serves. Clients work with senior practitioners who understand both the financial mechanics and the human dimensions of careers at the highest level.

From day-to-day business management through to royalty administration, tax planning and family office services, GRF provides an integrated advisory relationship rather than a collection of disconnected transactions. The firm's longevity reflects the trust placed in it by successive generations of entertainment industry talent.

Services

  • Business Management

    Comprehensive day-to-day financial management for entertainment professionals, covering cash flow, bill payment, budgeting and financial reporting tailored to the rhythms of a career in the arts or sport.

  • Tax Services

    Tax planning and compliance for individuals, entities and estates operating across multiple states and, where applicable, international jurisdictions, with strategies suited to irregular income structures.

  • Royalty Administration & Examinations

    Administration of royalty income streams and participation examinations to verify that clients receive accurate and complete payments under their recording, publishing, film and other entertainment agreements.

  • Family Office Services

    Wealth stewardship, estate planning support and financial coordination for high-net-worth entertainment families, bringing institutional rigour to the management of multigenerational assets.
