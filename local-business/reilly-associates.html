About Rivetti Financial

Specialists who understand the physician's financial journey

Rivetti Financial specialises in financial planning and insurance for physicians. We understand the unique challenges physicians face — long training periods, high debt loads, and careers that depend entirely on personal health and specialty capability.

  • Specialty-specific own-occupation disability coverage
  • No medical evaluation required to obtain coverage
  • Discounted rates for residents and fellows in training
  • Full financial planning — not just insurance
  • Focused exclusively on physicians and their financial needs
