How Ashcap works with business owners

  • Business Brokerage
    Full-service representation for owners selling their business. Ashcap manages valuation, buyer identification and outreach, negotiations, and the full closing process. The goal is a successful sale at the right price, with the right buyer.
  • Exit Planning
    For owners who are 1 to 3 years out from a planned sale, exit planning sets the conditions for a stronger outcome. Ashcap works with you to get the business structured, documented, and positioned to attract serious buyers and support a clean due diligence process.
  • Industries Served
    Ashcap works with owners across franchises, home health, education, staffing and recruiting, and IT services. Sector-specific experience means the team understands what buyers in each industry look for and how to present a business accordingly.
  • From Planning Through to Final Signature
    The Ashcap process does not hand you off mid-deal. The same team that starts your engagement is with you at closing. That continuity matters when the stakes are high and every detail of the deal counts.
