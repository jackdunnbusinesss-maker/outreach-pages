About McIntyre Property

Local People,
Real Knowledge

McIntyre Property has been part of the Canberra community for years — not as a franchise with a head office somewhere else, but as a locally owned agency that actually lives and works in the suburbs we sell.

We're based in Greenway, in the heart of Tuggeranong, and we service Belconnen, Woden, and Weston Creek too. That means we understand how the ACT property market behaves — the cycles, the government-sector demand, the seasonal quirks, and which pockets are quietly outperforming the rest.
