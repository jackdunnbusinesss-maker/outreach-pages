International Strategy & Technology Consulting

From strategy to implementation

Eraneos is an international strategy, transformation, and technology consulting group. We combine deep industry expertise with an in-depth knowledge of technology to help organisations achieve their goals — from high-level strategy through to full delivery.

Eraneos works across financial services, transportation and logistics, energy and utilities, life sciences, public sector, and automotive defence. The group understands the challenges each industry faces and ensures organisations make the most of opportunities created by digitalisation — whether that's building a new data platform, executing a transformation programme, or defining a long-term corporate strategy.

The firm's strength lies in combining fundamental industry knowledge with technical capability. Eraneos doesn't hand over a strategy deck and walk away. The group supports clients from the initial diagnostic all the way through to change that sticks — with specialist practices across corporate strategy, customer excellence, organisational transformation, data and AI, sourcing and IT advisory, and cybersecurity.

Operating internationally across Europe and beyond, Eraneos brings diverse perspectives and cross-sector experience to every engagement, ensuring clients benefit from thinking that goes beyond their own industry.
