Equipment built for the work that moves the world

Caterpillar Inc is the world's leading manufacturer of construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, industrial gas turbines, and diesel-electric locomotives. Founded in 1925, Caterpillar supports customers in more than 190 countries.

Caterpillar was founded in 1925 through the merger of the Holt Manufacturing Company and C.L. Best Tractor Co. in Peoria, Illinois. Nearly a century of engineering and field refinement has produced a product range that now spans everything from compact excavators to ultra-class mining trucks, sold and supported through the world's largest dealer network in the industry.

Every piece of Caterpillar equipment is backed by a global parts and service infrastructure. The Cat dealer network operates across more than 190 countries, providing on-site support, planned maintenance programmes, and rapid parts availability that keeps jobsites moving. Complementing the hardware, Cat Financial offers flexible equipment financing, leasing, and insurance solutions designed to match the capital cycles of contractors, miners, and infrastructure owners.

Caterpillar's customers operate across construction, road building, mining, quarrying, waste management, forestry, energy, rail, and marine industries. Whether they are building a city interchange, extracting ore at an open-cut mine, or managing a large landfill operation, the machinery, engines, and support services are designed to reduce total cost of ownership and maximise productive uptime on the ground.

What we deliver for our customers

  • Construction Machinery

    A comprehensive range of excavators, dozers, motor graders, wheel loaders, skid steer loaders, and articulated trucks built for civil construction, earthmoving, and road-building projects of every scale.
