Australia's Leading Pharmacy Network

Powering independent pharmacies across Australia

Sigma Healthcare is a leading network of independent and franchised pharmacies across Australia, delivering dispensary services, health services, private label brands, and end-to-end supply chain and logistics support.

Sigma Healthcare supports independent and franchised pharmacies across Australia with the services, technology, and supply chain infrastructure they need to compete and grow. As one of the country's leading pharmacy networks, Sigma combines national scale with local pharmacy expertise — giving member pharmacies access to resources they wouldn't have on their own.

The business operates across the full pharmacy value chain — from supply network and logistics through to dispensary technology, health services programs, and private label and exclusive brands. Pharmacies in the Sigma network benefit from competitive pricing, robust supply reliability, and a partner invested in their success.

With data and technology increasingly shaping how pharmacies operate and compete, Sigma provides the platforms and tools that help pharmacists focus on patient care rather than back-office complexity.

What Sigma Healthcare provides

  • Data & Technology
    Technology platforms and data services that help pharmacies manage their operations more efficiently — from dispensary software through to analytics that support better business decisions.
  • Dispensary Services
    Dispensary support services designed to help pharmacists deliver accurate, efficient dispensing while managing compliance requirements and keeping up with changing scripts volumes.
  • Health Services
    Health services programs that enable pharmacies to expand their clinical offering — supporting pharmacists to deliver vaccination, medication management, and other patient-facing services.
  • Supply Network & Logistics
    A national supply chain and logistics network that ensures pharmacies receive the products they need, when they need them — backed by strong supplier relationships and reliable distribution infrastructure.
  • Private Label & Exclusive Brands
    A range of private label and exclusive branded products that give Sigma network pharmacies a competitive edge and the ability to offer differentiated products their customers can't find elsewhere.
