Who We Are

Health & Finance Integrated is an award-winning specialist financial planning practice. We work with clients across wealth preservation and management, succession planning, strategic investment advice, taxation strategy, and retirement planning.

Our focus is people who need advice that goes beyond the standard — those managing a TPD payout, preparing for retirement on their own terms, families investing with discipline and care, and carers establishing Special Disability Trusts for a loved one.

We take the time to understand your health circumstances, your family situation, and your long-term goals before making any recommendation. That's the only way to give advice worth following.

What we can help you with

  • Post-TPD & Compensation Advice

    If your TPD claim has been approved, the decisions you make next have long-term consequences. We map the tax treatment, super access, cash flow, and investment steps so you can move forward with clarity and confidence.

  • Tax-Smart Investing & Portfolio Management

    Goal-based portfolios built with a clear strategy and reviewed regularly. We coordinate super and non-super investments, explain the rationale behind every move, and keep your plan on track as life changes.

  • Pre-Retirement & Retirement Income Planning

    Whether retirement is two years away or on the horizon, we help you structure your income, super, and entitlements so you're not leaving money on the table or making decisions you can't reverse.

  • Age Pension & Centrelink Strategy

    Centrelink rules are complex and the interactions with your super and assets can be costly if you get them wrong. We develop strategies that maximise your entitlements within the rules.

  • Special Disability Trusts & Protective Trusts

    For families planning for a loved one with a disability, we advise on the structure and establishment of Special Disability Trusts and Protective Trusts — giving you a framework that protects their future without jeopardising government support.

  • Financial Services Guides & Ongoing Reviews

    We provide clear documentation of our services, fees, and obligations. Regular client reviews keep your financial plan current as your circumstances evolve.
