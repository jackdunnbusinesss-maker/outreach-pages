5-minute strategy walkthrough — wealth management pipeline approach

Morgan Stanley is one of the world's leading financial services firms — operating across wealth management, institutional securities, and investment management globally. The wealth management division serves individuals and families from the Access Investing digital platform ($5,000 minimum) through to the full Financial Adviser relationship ($250,000+). The scale of Morgan Stanley's investment banking operation creates a research and alternative investment access advantage that no boutique or regional wealth manager can match.

The challenge is that HNW prospects evaluating wealth managers conduct extensive research before engaging an adviser — they're reading comparison content, evaluating platforms, and assessing which institution's research capabilities and investment access best match their needs. Morgan Stanley's differentiation — institutional intelligence, alternative investment access, coordinated financial planning — needs to be visible in that research phase. The strategy we've built covers how to generate qualified Financial Adviser inquiries from the HNW and business owner segments most likely to convert.

What we'd build for Morgan Stanley

  • HNW Audience Architecture
    LinkedIn and Facebook targeting across HNW wealth segments — mass affluent ($250K+), HNW ($2M+), and business owner pre-liquidity — with segment-specific content for each buyer journey stage. Global reach with specific weighting for US and Asia-Pacific markets.
  • Thought Leadership Pipeline
    LinkedIn Sponsored Content distributing Morgan Stanley research and market perspectives to C-suite executives, PE partners, and senior finance professionals globally — building adviser inquiry intent before it surfaces as a search.
  • Business Owner Pre-Liquidity
    LinkedIn targeting business owners and founders at scale-up stage — pre-liquidity planning content: "What happens to your wealth when you sell your business?" — a track that Merrill Lynch and UBS are not consistently running.
  • Access Investing Growth
    Google and Facebook for the mass affluent segment — Access Investing digital platform acquisition at the $5,000–$100,000 investable range. Building the pipeline of clients who grow into full Financial Adviser relationships over time.
