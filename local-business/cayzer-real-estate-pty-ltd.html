A local partnership, a wider reach

Buxton Port Phillip has joined forces with Cayzer Real Estate to form Buxton | Cayzer — a co-branded entity serving the Port Melbourne and Albert Park communities.

As part of a phased transition, Buxton Port Melbourne has moved into Cayzer's larger premises on Bay Street, while the Montague Street office continues to trade under the new co-branded identity. Both locations will operate this way over the next 6–12 months.

The result is a team of more than 50 property professionals across four strategic locations, combining Cayzer's local service legacy with the Buxton network's broader strength.
