Real Assets Advisory · Melbourne & Sydney

Advisory and investment across real estate, infrastructure and energy

Planum Partners is an independent advisory and investment firm focused on real assets. We work with institutional investors, funds, developers and corporates across Australia and internationally.

Planum Partners operates across the full spectrum of real assets, from real estate and social infrastructure through to energy and large-scale project finance. Our advisory practice draws on deep transactional experience and long-standing relationships with institutional counterparties across the region.

Our funds business covers real estate and social infrastructure, providing investors with structured exposure to assets that combine stable long-term returns with genuine social utility. On the advisory side, we work at the intersection of capital markets and project delivery, helping clients arrange and structure capital for complex transactions that require both technical understanding and market credibility.

We hold Australian Financial Services Licence 479535. Our Melbourne office sits at Level 37, 80 Collins Street. Our Sydney office is at Level 34 Gateway, 1 Macquarie Place. Both teams work across origination, structuring and execution, giving clients consistent coverage through the full transaction lifecycle.

Our capabilities

  • Advisory

    Independent advisory across real estate, infrastructure and energy. We advise on transactions, capital structures and strategic positioning for institutional investors, funds, developers and corporates.

  • Funds

  • Capital Arrangement
