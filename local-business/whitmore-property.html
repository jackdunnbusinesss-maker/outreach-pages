Since 2005, Whitmore has been identifying and securing high-potential commercial property investments for wholesale investors across Australia. Over two decades, we have built a track record of delivering strong, consistent returns while maintaining the capital security our investors rely on.

Our approach prioritises communication — investors are informed at every step. Sophisticated strategies, simplified. Whether through single-asset schemes or portfolio-managed funds, we create structures that are designed for clarity and built for performance.

We also actively pursue opportunities in alternative commercial assets, including holiday parks and lifestyle communities. Through strategic partnerships and deep market insight, we unlock hidden value, enhance asset performance, and deliver strong, sustainable returns.

What we do

  • Funds Management

    We manage 20 established funds across the commercial property sector, providing wholesale investors with structured, managed exposure to high-potential assets. Our fund management approach is built on disciplined acquisition, active oversight, and transparent reporting.

  • Asset Management (Advisory)

    Strategic advisory across the full asset lifecycle. We work alongside property owners and investors to maximise long-term growth, improve asset performance, and protect capital across market cycles.

  • Property Management

    Proactive day-to-day management of commercial property investments. We take a hands-on approach to tenancy, maintenance, and performance — so investors can focus on returns, not operations.
