Charity Fundraising Consultants · Est. 1998

Fundraising expertise built over decades, not months

Wootton George Consulting works exclusively with UK charities and not-for-profit organisations, bringing genuine depth and a long track record to every brief we take on.

Since 1998 we have helped charities across the UK raise funds through National Lottery bids, charitable trust applications, capital appeals, corporate partnerships and individual giving programmes. Our team carries more than 300 years of combined experience, with each consultant averaging over 15 years working directly in the sector.

Seventy percent of our work comes from repeat clients or direct referrals. That figure matters to us more than any other because it tells us what our clients think once a project is over. We are known for being affordable, straightforward and genuinely invested in the causes we support.

Whether you are a small local charity writing your first Lottery application or an established organisation planning a major capital appeal, we fit around your capacity and your budget. We cover the full fundraising cycle, from donor research and prospect identification right through to copywriting, legacy fundraising and digital fundraising strategy.

What we do

  • Statutory and Trust Funding

    National Lottery bids, charitable trust applications and bid writing across a wide range of statutory and grant-making bodies. We write clearly, argue compellingly and meet deadlines.

  • Capital Appeals

    Consultancy for capital building appeals including church and heritage projects. We help you structure the campaign, set realistic targets and approach the right funders at the right time.

  • Individual and Major Giving

    Donor research, individual giving programmes, major gifts strategy and legacy fundraising. We help you find your most committed supporters and build lasting relationships with them.

  • Corporate and Digital Fundraising

    Corporate partnership development and digital fundraising support to diversify your income mix and reach audiences beyond your existing donor base.

  • Fundraising Audits and Copywriting

    Independent fundraising audits to identify gaps and opportunities in your current approach. Professional fundraising copywriting for appeals, impact reports and donor communications.
