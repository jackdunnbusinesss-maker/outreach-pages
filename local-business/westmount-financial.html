About Westmount Financial

Westmount Financial is a long-established, Perth-based financial advisory firm with a history stretching back to 1977. For nearly five decades, the practice has helped Western Australian families navigate the complexities of personal finance — through periods of economic growth, market disruption, and structural change.

Most people don't need more financial information. They need someone to cut through the noise, make sense of where they stand, and help them build a plan that reflects their actual life. That's the foundation of Westmount's approach — grounding decisions in values and priorities, not generic benchmarks.

The real value of good advice shows up over time: in the ongoing conversations, the course corrections when markets become uncomfortable, and the clarity to move forward with confidence. Less second-guessing. Less complexity. More momentum toward the retirement and lifestyle you're working toward.
