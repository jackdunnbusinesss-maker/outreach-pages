Who We Are

Corporate Carbon is in the business of creating new solutions for carbon market participation. We ensure emissions reductions, carbon sequestration, and co-benefits for the environment, individuals, and businesses across Australia.

Our purpose is to reduce emissions by the gigatonne by accelerating global transformation and transition to a decarbonised economy. As a leading Australian multi-sector developer, we were actively engaged in building the local Carbon Farming Initiative offsets industry from its very inception — a position of first-mover knowledge and relationships that no newcomer can replicate.

Across more than 100 projects under the Emissions Reduction Fund, we have delivered 15 million ACCUs to the Clean Energy Regulator and commercial clients. That track record underpins everything we do today: project development, trading, advisory, and agribusiness incubation.
