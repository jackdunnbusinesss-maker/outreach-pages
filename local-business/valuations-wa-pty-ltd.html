Our valuation services

  • Property Valuations

    Independent, certified valuations for residential and commercial properties across Perth and regional WA. Every report is prepared by a qualified, registered valuer with no conflicts of interest.

  • Family Law Valuations

    Court-admissible property valuations for family law proceedings and asset settlement matters. Prepared to the standards required by the Family Court of Australia, delivered with the sensitivity these situations demand.

  • Capital Gains Tax Valuations

    Pre-sale and retrospective valuations to establish market value for capital gains tax purposes. Compliant with ATO requirements and prepared to withstand scrutiny from your accountant or the ATO.

  • Retrospective Tax Valuations

    Historical market valuations dated back to a specific point in time. Commonly required for estate administration, CGT events, and tax audit situations where the value at a past date needs to be established.

  • Stamp Duty / Related Party Transfer Valuations

    Independent valuations required by the WA Office of State Revenue for stamp duty assessments, related party transfers, and property transactions where an arms-length sale price is not available.

  • Strata Building Insurance Valuations

    Replacement cost assessments for strata buildings to ensure body corporates carry adequate insurance coverage. Prepared in accordance with strata legislation requirements and insurer guidelines.

  • SMSF Valuations

    Independent market valuations for property held within a self-managed superannuation fund. Required annually for SMSF compliance and prepared to meet ATO and ASIC reporting standards.
