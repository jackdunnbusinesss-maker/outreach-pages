About Us

Local Knowledge. Independent Advice.

Valuations WA Pty Ltd is Perth's leading property valuation firm, built on a foundation of deep local market knowledge and decades of on-the-ground experience across Western Australia.

Every report we produce is fully independent and objective — free from the conflicts of interest that can compromise valuations tied to sales, finance, or insurance commissions. Our valuers are engaged solely to provide accurate, defensible assessments that hold up in court, before the ATO, or in any professional context.

From residential properties in the suburbs to complex commercial assets, our team understands the nuances of the Perth market and delivers valuations with the precision and credibility that solicitors, accountants, trustees, and investors rely on.
