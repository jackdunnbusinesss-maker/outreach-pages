Valuation services

  • Family Law Valuations
    Independent valuations prepared for property settlement proceedings. Reports are structured to meet the requirements of family law matters and are prepared by qualified CPVs.
  • Capital Gains Tax Valuations
    Accurate market value assessments for CGT purposes, prepared in accordance with ATO requirements and ready for submission with your tax return.
  • Retrospective Tax Valuations
    Historical property valuations dated to a specific past point in time, used for establishing cost base, pre-CGT status, or other retrospective tax obligations.
  • Stamp Duty & Related Party Transfers
    Valuations required by the Office of State Revenue for dutiable transactions, including transfers between related parties and trust restructures.
  • Strata Building Insurance Valuations
    Replacement cost assessments for strata scheme buildings, used to ensure insurance coverage accurately reflects current rebuild costs.
  • SMSF Valuations
    Compliant independent valuations of SMSF property assets prepared to meet the requirements of the ATO and your SMSF auditor.
