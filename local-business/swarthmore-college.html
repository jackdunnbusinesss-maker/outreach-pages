About Swarthmore

Swarthmore College is a place where people with good ideas come together to make great ones. Founded in 1864 by members of the Religious Society of Friends, the college has spent more than 160 years preparing graduates to lead with purpose, rigour, and integrity.

Located on a beautifully landscaped campus just outside Philadelphia, Swarthmore offers the academic intensity of a research university within the close-knit environment of a small liberal arts college. Faculty teach their own classes. Students engage in real research. Conversations don't end when class does.

As a QuestBridge partner, Swarthmore is committed to ensuring that access is not determined by income — meeting 100% of demonstrated financial need for all admitted students.
