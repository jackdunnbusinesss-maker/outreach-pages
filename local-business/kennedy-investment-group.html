One team. One plan.
Every dimension of your wealth.

Kennedy Investment Group serves corporate executives, business owners, affluent families, professional athletes, and nonprofit organizations with a coordinated, team-based approach to financial planning built for the long term.

Since 2004, Kennedy Investment Group has worked from a simple premise: the best financial outcomes happen when all the pieces of your financial life are planned together, not separately. Retirement, estate, succession, asset protection, and multi-generational wealth are not isolated decisions. They affect each other. Planning them in silos creates gaps.

The firm's team-based model is built to eliminate those gaps. Rather than relying on a single advisor who hands off parts of your plan to outside specialists, Kennedy Investment Group coordinates every discipline internally, giving clients a complete and consistent financial picture at every stage.

The clients Kennedy Investment Group serves share one thing in common: the complexity of their financial situations demands more than a transactional relationship. Corporate executives with layered compensation packages, business owners planning an eventual exit, athletes with concentrated earning windows, and families managing wealth across generations all benefit from the same thing: a plan that accounts for all of it.
