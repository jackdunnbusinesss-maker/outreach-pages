Our Story

A century of knowing East Gippsland.

King & Heath opened its doors in 1923 — before the Great Depression, before World War II, before most of the housing stock in East Gippsland was even built. What started as a single office in Bairnsdale has grown across three generations, becoming the most enduring independent real estate practice in the region.

"We're not a franchise that rotates agents through. These are our people, our families, our towns. We've been here long before the property boom and we'll be here long after."

Unlike the major corporate groups, King & Heath operates as a proud independent member of the First National Real Estate network — Australia's largest independent real estate group with over 450 offices. That means you get the reach and resources of a national network, backed by agents who actually live in East Gippsland.

Three generations of the family have managed properties from Bairnsdale to Mallacoota, seen markets rise and fall, and watched their clients' children grow up and buy homes of their own. That kind of continuity isn't something you find on a property app — it's something you earn over a century.
