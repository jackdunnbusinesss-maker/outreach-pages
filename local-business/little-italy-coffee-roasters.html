About us

Little Italy Coffee Roasters is a Sydney-based specialty roastery built on a simple idea: coffee done properly is worth the effort. We source carefully, roast with precision, and share what we know through training and direct access to our roasting facility.

Our barista training programmes run from beginner through to intermediate, giving home brewers and aspiring cafe staff the practical skills to back themselves at the machine. The Roasting Room and Tasting Lab is available for visits when you want to see the whole process end to end.

Whether you're stocking a cafe, building a home setup, or learning from scratch, we're here for all of it.
