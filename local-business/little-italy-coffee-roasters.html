Barista Training

Learn the Craft

Whether you're picking up a portafilter for the first time or refining your technique, LICR's training courses are built around the art and science of great coffee.

Introductory
Introductory Barista Course

Start your coffee journey with the fundamentals — espresso extraction, milk texturing, and the core principles behind a great cup. Suitable for home enthusiasts and those considering a career in coffee.

Advanced
Advanced Barista Course

Deepen your technical knowledge and refine your workflow. Covers advanced espresso theory, dialling in, palate development, and consistency under pressure — for those serious about the craft.

Experiences

Come Inside

Coffee is best understood when you see it being made. LICR offers two immersive on-site experiences in Sydney.

On-Site Experiences

Roasting Room & Tasting Lab

Step into the LICR Roasting Room for a behind-the-scenes look at the roasting process — where the science of heat meets the art of flavour development. Then move through to the Tasting Lab to explore the finished product: cupping, sensory evaluation, and guided tastings led by the LICR team.

  • Roasting Room — see the full roasting process up close
  • Tasting Lab — guided cupping and sensory sessions
  • Hosted by the LICR team in Sydney, NSW
  • Available for individuals, groups, and trade visitors
