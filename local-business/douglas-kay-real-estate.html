Tips & Insights

Practical advice from people who know this market

We publish guides for buyers and sellers based on what we see working on the ground in Melbourne's west. Not generic advice — what actually matters here.

For Buyers
Handy tips for buying property
  • Prepare a wish list with your must-have features — location, bedrooms, parking — before you start inspecting
  • Organise loan pre-approval early; it strengthens your negotiating position and speeds up the process considerably
  • Line up a conveyancer or solicitor before you need one — you want to be ready to move on a contract quickly
  • Budget beyond the mortgage: maintenance, council rates, land tax, insurance, and any strata fees all add up
  • Inspect as many properties as possible — it sharpens your eye for value and tells you when something is fairly priced
  • Building and pest inspections are worth every dollar for the peace of mind they provide
  • Always do a pre-settlement inspection on the day to confirm the property is exactly as per the contract
Read the full guide →
For Sellers
Important hints for selling property
  • Presentation matters — fresh paint, tidy gardens, minor repairs, and a professional clean can add real value without major spend
  • Create a task checklist early so nothing slips through the cracks between now and settlement
  • Professional styling works especially well for vacant properties; it helps buyers picture themselves in the space
  • Be specific about what's included in the sale — light fittings, blinds, appliances — to avoid disputes later
  • Consider getting your own building and pest inspection upfront; addressing issues before listing strengthens your position
  • Timing the market varies by suburb and property type — your agent is best placed to advise on this
  • Choose your sales method carefully: private treaty, auction, and public tender each suit different properties and goals
