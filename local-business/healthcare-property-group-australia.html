About Healthcare Property Group

Healthcare Property Group is Australia's leading specialist in healthcare real estate. Unlike general commercial agents, every property, every transaction, and every advisory engagement is focused exclusively on the healthcare sector.

From individual practitioners seeking a consulting room to large healthcare operators managing complex facility portfolios — HPG brings sector-specific expertise that generalist agencies simply cannot match. Our team understands the regulatory, operational, and financial nuances that make healthcare property a distinct asset class.

With offices in Melbourne and Sydney and active listings across Altona North, Boronia, Box Hill, Caulfield South, Southbank, and Werribee, HPG operates at the intersection of real estate and healthcare — delivering full-service capability under one roof.
