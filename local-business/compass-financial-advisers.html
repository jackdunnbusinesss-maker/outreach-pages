About Compass Financial Advisers

At Compass Financial Advisers, we believe that great advice stems from a team with the right individuals — those who not only possess the necessary skills but also share a genuine passion for making a meaningful difference.

Located in Canberra, we are committed to meeting the complex and changing insurance and financial planning needs of our clients. Our team of specialists are dedicated to providing leading service backed by deep knowledge of the Australian insurance and superannuation landscape.

We operate as a Corporate Authorised Representative (453944) of Matrix Planning Solutions Limited, giving our clients access to a robust compliance framework and institutional-grade resources — with the personal attention of a boutique practice.

What we do

  • Financial Planning

    Comprehensive financial planning tailored to your goals — helping you navigate your financial security and get on the right track for the outcomes you want.

  • Insurance Services

    Personal insurance that keeps you protected through life's unexpected events — ensuring your assets, your family, and your future remain secure against premature death or a major medical event.
