Committed to human and Christian education in the Lasallian tradition

St Michael's College is a Catholic secondary school in Adelaide committed to the human and academic development of every student. Guided by the Lasallian tradition, the College nurtures faith, knowledge, and character across a diverse and inclusive community.

St Michael's College is a Catholic school in the Lasallian tradition, a heritage rooted in the work of Saint John Baptist de La Salle, who established a model of education built on reaching students of every background and helping them flourish. That founding mission shapes everything about how the College operates today, from its curriculum design to its pastoral care structures.

The College offers a diverse academic curriculum alongside a structured co-curricular programme, giving students the opportunity to develop beyond the classroom. Pastoral support is embedded across the school through counselling services and allied health service provision, ensuring that students are supported not just academically but in their overall wellbeing. The College also operates a Primary campus, extending its educational approach to younger students within the same community.

St Michael's serves students and families who are looking for a school that takes both academic rigour and the formation of good character seriously. Student Services and counselling are available throughout the school year, and the reception team is available to assist current and prospective families with any enquiries about enrolment, services, or College life.

How we support our students

  • Student Services

    A dedicated Student Services team provides guidance and practical support for students navigating academic requirements, subject selection, and school life throughout their secondary years.

  • Counselling and Allied Health

    Professional counselling services and allied health service provision are integrated into the College's pastoral care framework, supporting the mental, emotional, and physical wellbeing of every student.

  • Diverse Curriculum

    A broad and diverse academic curriculum gives students the flexibility to pursue their individual strengths and interests, preparing them for further study and life beyond school.

  • Co-Curricular Activities

    A rich programme of co-curricular activities complements classroom learning, encouraging students to develop leadership, teamwork, creativity, and confidence through sport, the arts, and community involvement.
