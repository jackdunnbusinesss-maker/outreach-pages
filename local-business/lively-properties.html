Local Experience

The Peninsula, beyond
your front door

Staying lively means more than a beautiful home. We've partnered with the Peninsula's best venues so your guests arrive with a curated itinerary, not a blank map.

🍷
Ten Minutes by Tractor
One of the Peninsula's most acclaimed wineries and restaurants — fine dining surrounded by working vineyards. An unmissable lunch stop.
🖼️
PT Leo Estate
Sculpture park, world-class wine, and a restaurant with views across Port Phillip Bay. Art, food, and the Peninsula in one afternoon.
🧖
Aurora Spa & Bathhouse
Peninsula-born day spa offering thermal circuits, massage, and restorative treatments — the perfect wind-down after a day at the beach.
🍝
Zonzo Estate
Italian country dining in the Yarra Valley — handmade pasta, wood-fired pizza, and sprawling vineyard views just beyond the Peninsula hills.
⛳
The Dunes Golf Links
Championship links golf cut through coastal dunes — consistently rated among Victoria's top courses. Tee times available for Lively guests.
🌊
Peninsula Beaches
Ocean Beach, Rye Front Beach, Sorrento Front — calm bays and rugged surf coasts, all within minutes of your holiday home.
