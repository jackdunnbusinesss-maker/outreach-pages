About GDS

Group Development Services is a full-service civil engineering and surveying firm that has been operating across NSW since 1989. Over more than three decades we've built a track record working alongside property developers, residential builders, commercial operators, and private landowners.

We handle planning, project management, civil engineering, and surveying in-house, which means fewer handoffs and a cleaner line from concept through to completion. No compartmentalised advice that doesn't connect. One team that understands the whole picture.

If you're at the start of a project or stuck somewhere in the middle, we're the kind of firm that knows what to do with a complicated brief.
