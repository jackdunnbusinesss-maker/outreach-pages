Our Story

Built right here in Rocky, from the ground up

In 1986, Pat O'Driscoll opened a real estate agency with two staff members and a 40m² office on Bolsover Street. The idea was simple: do the job properly, treat people fairly, and the rest would follow.

Today that same office supports a team of 32 professionals covering residential sales, property management, commercial, rural, and land across Central Queensland. Still locally owned. Still on Bolsover Street.

The team manages hundreds of properties, handles sales from Gracemere to Yeppoon and Emu Park, and has built relationships with clients who keep coming back for decades. That's not a marketing line — it's what happens when you do the little things right, consistently, for nearly four decades.

"We believe it is the little things that make the difference."
