About Turrell Building

Turrell Building has been constructing homes across Sydney for over 30 years. What started as a local building business has grown into one of the region's most trusted names in custom residential construction — without losing the personal approach that made us what we are.

We specialise in custom homes, renovations, and first-floor additions across Sydney's Hills District, North Shore, and surrounding suburbs. Our team works closely with clients from initial design right through to handover, keeping communication clear and the process straightforward.

Alongside Turrell Building, our family of businesses includes Turrell Homes and Turrell Pools — giving clients a single, trusted partner for all aspects of their property.
