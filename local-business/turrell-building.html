Who We Are

Over Three Decades of Craft

Turrell Building has been constructing Sydney’s finest homes for over 30 years, earning a reputation for quality that stands long after handover. Based in Galston, NSW, we specialise in custom homes, renovations, and first-floor additions throughout Sydney’s most desirable suburbs.
