Nikulski Financial has been helping individuals, families, and small businesses build and protect financial security since 2009. As fiduciaries, the team is legally required to act in your best interest.

Nikulski Financial, Inc. is a fee-based financial planning firm staffed by Certified Financial Planners based in Bettendorf, Iowa. Since 2009, the firm has focused on comprehensive personal financial planning, investment management, and small business planning for clients who want a trusted advisor in their corner.

The fiduciary standard is central to how Nikulski Financial operates. As CFP professionals, the team is held to a legal and ethical obligation to recommend what is genuinely best for each client, not what generates the highest commission. That distinction matters when you are making decisions about your retirement, your investments, or your business finances.

Whether you are building a plan from scratch, managing a growing investment portfolio, or navigating the financial complexity of running a small business, Nikulski Financial offers the personal attention and professional depth that larger institutions rarely provide.
