About Nikulski Financial

Nikulski Financial are Certified Financial Planners (CFP® professionals) committed to always acting in your best interests. In practice since 2009, we've built our firm around a simple belief: great financial planning starts with understanding who you are, what you care about, and what you're building toward.

Most financial plans look the same because they're built from the same templates. Ours aren't. We take the time to understand your unique purpose, passion, and joy — and build a comprehensive strategy that reflects your actual life, not a generic model. As fiduciaries, we're bound to act in your interest, not ours.

Based in Bettendorf, Iowa, we serve individuals, families, and small business owners who want a financial partner who truly knows their situation — and keeps their best interests at the centre of every decision.
