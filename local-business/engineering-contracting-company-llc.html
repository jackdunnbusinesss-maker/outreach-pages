A group built to deliver complex projects across construction and engineering

ECC Group brings together a collective of specialised sister companies to deliver innovative, industry-leading solutions across construction and engineering disciplines throughout the UAE and the wider region.

ECC Group is structured as a group of specialised sister companies, each focused on a distinct area of construction and engineering. This model allows clients to access deep sector expertise without the limitations of a single-discipline firm.

The group was founded on the belief that complex built-environment challenges require more than generalist contractors. By bringing complementary capabilities under one umbrella, ECC Group is able to coordinate across disciplines and deliver integrated outcomes for large-scale and technically demanding projects.

Operating across the UAE and the broader Middle East, ECC Group serves clients in industries where precision, compliance, and construction quality are non-negotiable.

What ECC Group brings to a project

  • Group Structure

  • Industry-Leading Solutions

  • Integrated Delivery

    By coordinating across its sister companies, ECC Group can manage multi-discipline engagements under a single point of accountability, reducing complexity for clients.
