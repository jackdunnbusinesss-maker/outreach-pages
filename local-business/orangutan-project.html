Our partners

World-class conservation partners

The Orangutan Project works alongside specialist organisations with decades of on-the-ground experience across Borneo and Sumatra.

Frankfurt Zoological Society

One of the world's leading wildlife conservation organisations, active in protecting biodiversity-rich ecosystems.

Centre for Orangutan Protection

Dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and releasing orangutans, and combating the illegal wildlife trade.

Borneo Nature Foundation

Long-term research and conservation work in Central Kalimantan, protecting peatland forests critical for orangutan survival.

Who we are

A registered Australian charity fighting for orangutans

The Orangutan Project is a registered Australian charity saving orangutans from habitat loss, poaching, and human-orangutan conflict. We work with international conservation partners in the field where it matters most.

  • Registered Australian charity — ABN 92 607 879 345
  • All donations of $2 or more are tax deductible
  • International field partnerships across Borneo and Sumatra
  • Focus on habitat protection, rescue, and rehabilitation
  • Addressing poaching and human-orangutan conflict
