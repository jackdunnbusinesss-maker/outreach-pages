Saving orangutans
from extinction

Orangutans are disappearing at a rate the world cannot afford to ignore. The Orangutan Project works directly in the field to protect these animals, defend their habitat, and ensure future generations have a chance to survive. Your support makes that work possible.

Orangutans share more than 96% of their DNA with humans, and they are running out of time. Habitat destruction, poaching, and human-orangutan conflict have pushed both Bornean and Sumatran orangutans to the edge. Vast tracts of rainforest continue to be cleared for agriculture and development, leaving orangutans without the forest they depend on for survival.

The Orangutan Project has been working since 2008 to change that. We partner with on-the-ground conservation organisations including the Frankfurt Zoological Society, the Centre for Orangutan Protection, and the Borneo Nature Foundation to protect critical habitat, run rescue and rehabilitation programmes, and address the human pressures that drive orangutans into danger.

We are a registered Australian charity. All donations of $2 or more are fully tax deductible. Every dollar given to The Orangutan Project is directed towards conservation work that has a measurable impact in the field. This is not a corporate campaign. It is a focused effort by people who understand the crisis and know how to respond to it.

How You Can Help

  • Adopt an Orangutan
    Symbolic adoption connects you directly to an individual animal in care. You receive updates on their progress and the knowledge that your monthly support is funding their rehabilitation and, where possible, their return to the wild.
  • Donate or Save Forest
    One-off or regular donations fund land protection, patrol operations, and rescue work. Forest preservation donations go directly towards securing and protecting critical orangutan habitat from clearing and encroachment.
  • Plant Forest
    Support active reforestation in degraded areas. Planting trees in and around existing habitat corridors helps reconnect fragmented forest patches and expand the space available for wild orangutans to survive and breed.
  • Wildlife Eco-Tours & Philanthropy
    Experience orangutan conservation first-hand through our wildlife eco-tours, or explore structured philanthropic giving and business supporter programmes designed for organisations that want their contribution to go further.
