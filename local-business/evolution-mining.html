About Evolution Mining

Inspired people creating a premier global gold company

Evolution Mining is one of Australia's leading gold producers, with six mines operating across Australia and Canada. We mine gold responsibly — prioritising the safety of our people, the integrity of our operations, and the wellbeing of the communities we work alongside.

Our approach is grounded in the principle that what we say, we do. And what we do, we deliver. That commitment runs from the mine floor to the boardroom — informing how we manage our assets, how we communicate with shareholders, and how we engage with the broader communities that host our operations.

Headquartered in Brisbane with offices in Sydney, Evolution is building for the long term — growing our resource base through exploration and disciplined acquisition while maintaining the financial strength to fund continued growth.
