Where every experience is yours for the taking

Melbourne Recital Centre is the city's world-class concert venue, home to the Elisabeth Murdoch Hall and the Primrose Potter Salon. From intimate recitals to large-scale performances, it is a space built for music to be heard at its finest.

Situated in the heart of Melbourne, the Recital Centre stands as one of Australia's premier dedicated live music venues. The building was designed with acoustic excellence at its core, allowing every seat in Elisabeth Murdoch Hall to offer an exceptional listening experience regardless of the program on stage.

The Centre presents a year-round calendar of concerts and performances spanning classical, chamber, contemporary, and cross-genre programs. It also operates the Primrose Potter Salon, an intimate performance space suited to smaller ensembles and more personal audience encounters with artists.

Beyond performances, Melbourne Recital Centre welcomes individuals, businesses, and organisations to hire its spaces for events, while its membership program offers regular attendees a deeper connection to the venue and its programming.

What the Centre offers

  • Elisabeth Murdoch Hall

    The Centre's main performance hall, designed for world-class acoustic quality. The venue for major concerts and headline performances on the Melbourne Recital Centre program.

  • Primrose Potter Salon

    An intimate space for chamber music, recitals, and smaller ensemble performances. The Salon offers audiences a closer, more personal concert experience.

  • Venue Hire

    Both halls and the Centre's facilities are available for hire by organisations and individuals for events, functions, and performances. Enquire via the Box Office for availability and packages.

  • Membership

    A membership program for regular attendees, offering a closer relationship with the Centre's programming and the Melbourne performing arts community.
