Healthcare recruitment and training, done right

Healthcare Australia is the country's leading provider of healthcare recruitment and training. From agency nursing and allied health through to permanent recruitment, international applications, and travel nursing — HCA connects the right people with the right roles.

Healthcare Australia has built its reputation as Australia's leading healthcare recruitment and training provider by genuinely understanding both the healthcare sector and the professionals who work within it. Hospitals, aged care facilities, community health organisations, and primary care providers rely on HCA to fill both urgent short-term and long-term permanent positions with qualified, reliable staff.

The business operates across multiple recruitment streams — from agency staffing that fills immediate roster gaps, through to permanent recruitment that helps organisations build lasting teams. HCA also supports international healthcare professionals looking to work in Australia, managing the complex registration and immigration process from overseas application through to placement.

With offices in Melbourne and Brisbane, and a national network of healthcare professionals, HCA is positioned to connect healthcare workers and employers wherever they are in Australia.

What Healthcare Australia provides

  • Healthcare Employment
    Agency and permanent healthcare employment solutions connecting qualified nurses, allied health professionals, and support workers with healthcare facilities across Australia.
  • International Applications
    Specialist support for international healthcare professionals looking to work in Australia — managing the registration, credentialling, and immigration process from overseas application through to first placement.
  • Travel Nursing
    Travel nursing placements that allow registered nurses to work across different locations and facilities in Australia — with flexible arrangements that suit both the professional and the employer.
  • Agency Staffing
    Agency staffing solutions for healthcare facilities that need to fill roster gaps quickly — with a large pool of qualified professionals available for short-notice shifts and extended contracts.
  • Permanent Recruitment
    Permanent recruitment services for healthcare organisations looking to build their teams for the long term — with thorough candidate screening and matching to find the right cultural and clinical fit.
