Renee (keepsitreel) has been telling the unfiltered truth about sports mom life to 43,000 people — the logistical chaos, the emotional investment, the referee arguments you regret immediately, and the deep pride of watching your kid do something they love. Her Gen X humor and no-drama honesty turned her into a voice that sports moms trust.

The Sideline Club is the community version of that. Not a parenting advice group, not a how-to resource — a place for women who are neck-deep in youth sports culture and want to be around other people who get the specific kind of tired, proud, and occasionally unhinged that comes with it.

If your weekend schedule is dictated by tournament brackets and you've eaten more concession stand hot dogs than you'd like to admit — this is your community.
