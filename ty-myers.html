Here's What Actually Works.

Going full-time on YouTube is a math problem, not a luck problem. Ty's worked backwards from the destination to build a clear, repeatable process for getting there:

1

Pick the right niche and validate it before you post 50 videos in the wrong direction

Most people skip this step. They pick what they're interested in rather than what the market will pay for. There's a way to find where those two things overlap — and that's where channels actually grow.

2

Build titles and thumbnails that actually get clicked

Your content quality doesn't matter if nobody clicks. Thumbnails and titles are the two levers with the highest return on time. Ty has analyzed what works across 9 million data points. You'll learn exactly what that looks like for your niche.

3

Post consistently enough to give the algorithm data without burning out

There's a sustainable cadence that fits a 40+ life — career, family, other obligations. You don't need to post every day. You need to post on a schedule you can actually hold for 12 months.

4

Know your monetization strategy before you hit the milestones

AdSense is one piece. Memberships, digital products, brand deals, coaching — the full-time creators combine multiple streams. You'll know exactly which ones fit your channel before you're ready to go full-time.
