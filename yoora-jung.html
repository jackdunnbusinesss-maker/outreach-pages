A system built for the worst-case week, not the ideal one.

Most study systems assume your week goes according to plan. Medical training doesn't. 8pm-8am night shifts, last-minute rotation schedule changes, days where you have nothing left — that's the real environment this has to work inside of.

The framework I teach was built specifically for this: structure that doesn't require ideal conditions to hold. You build for the hard week so the normal week is easy.
