The Process

Exactly How You Get From Here to Consistent Sales

The system that works when you have real expertise and don't want to build an audience from scratch on social media.

  1. 1

    Identify the exact problem you solve — and who's searching for it

    We match your expertise to a specific buyer who is actively looking for it online. Not "professionals" in general — the specific person typing specific searches who will pay for what you know.

  2. 2

    Build a lead magnet that shows up in search

    A checklist, short guide, or resource optimized for the exact search terms your buyer uses. This builds your email list from people who are already looking — no social media following required.

  3. 3

    Validate the product before you build it

    A simple sales page sent to your growing list. If people buy the pre-sale, you build. If they don't, you iterate before you've invested 80 hours. Most people have buyers before they finish building.

  4. 4

    Build the product (faster than you think)

    Using the framework I teach, most people have a launch-ready product in 3–6 weeks. Not a polished masterpiece — a useful, buyable product that solves the specific problem you identified in Step 1.

  5. 5

    Send the launch email sequence

    Three to five emails to your list. These do the selling. First-launch results from this process range from a few hundred dollars to several thousand — depending on list size and price point.

  6. 6

    Set up the ongoing system (2–3 hours per week)

    One email per week. One piece of search-optimized content per month. The list grows. The product sells. You maintain the system. That's it.
