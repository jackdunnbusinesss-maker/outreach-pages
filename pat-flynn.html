How it works

The exact path from zero to passive income — no steps skipped

Pat has done this publicly since 2008. Here's the condensed version of what actually moves the needle.

01

Find the problem worth solving — in a market that will pay for the solution

Not just "what are you passionate about" — that's bad advice. We identify the intersection of what you know, what your audience needs, and what people have already proven they'll buy.

02

Build the audience before you need it

Pat's model is audience first, offer second. You build the trust, the community, the email list — before you have something to sell. When the offer launches, the audience is already there.

03

Create and validate the offer with real people

Not six months of building. You test the offer in a smaller, faster format first. If people buy, you build it bigger. If they don't, you iterate before you've wasted the full investment.

04

Automate the sales system so income continues without you

Email sequences, evergreen funnels, partnerships. The offer keeps selling while you're creating new content, spending time with your family, or building the next thing.
