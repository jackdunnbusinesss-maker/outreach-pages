The Academic Comeback System

A structured 8-week process built around how exams actually work — not how school teaches you to prepare for them.

🔍

Weeks 1–2: Diagnose

Identify exactly which question types are dropping your marks — not just your weak topics. Those are different things, and fixing the right one changes results faster.

⚙️

Weeks 3–6: Apply

Implement retrieval practice across your subjects with weekly check-ins. Active recall, spaced repetition, and deliberate practice — applied to your actual exam syllabus.

🎯

Weeks 6–8: Perform

Targeted exam technique work: how mark schemes reward answers, how to structure under time pressure, how to pace across a full paper. Practice that mirrors the actual exam.
