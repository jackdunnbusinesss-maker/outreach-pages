Questions

Is this for moms with young kids or any age?

Most members have younger kids (toddlers through early school age), but the community welcomes moms at any stage. The themes — overwhelm, imperfection, marriage strain — don't expire when the kids grow up.

Is this just a venting group?

No. There's community support and humor, but Amanda also brings real frameworks and resources for making things better. The goal isn't just to commiserate — it's to actually help.

How active is the community?

Very. This isn't a ghost town. Between the live monthly calls, ongoing community conversation, and Amanda's active presence, it moves.

Do I need to be in a two-parent household to get value?

Not at all. Single moms, married moms, separated moms — the community is for all of them. The common thread is being a millennial mom who's done pretending.
