Common questions

Is this only for recently separated veterans?

No. People find this program years after separation when they realize they're still running on old patterns. Whether you separated last month or five years ago, the work is the same — you're just starting from a different place.

Do I have to be dealing with specific health or mental health challenges to benefit?

Not at all. This isn't a clinical program. It's about building a life you actually want to live — physically, mentally, and practically. You can be thriving in some areas and stuck in others. That's exactly who this is for.

Is this focused on the US military or does it apply internationally?

While Chivon's experience is rooted in the US context, the transition challenges she covers — identity, purpose, wellness, lifestyle design — are universal to military service regardless of country.

How is this different from standard VA resources or transition programs?

This is about building a life you're excited about — not just managing the transition. VA programs focus on benefits and employment. The Second Chapter System focuses on who you're becoming and how you actually want to live.

What's the time commitment?

You can work through the program at your own pace. The modules are designed to be completed over 8–10 weeks, but there's no expiration date and community access is ongoing.
