Questions

Is this for international travel or domestic trips too?

Both. The planning system and budget framework work for any trip. There are specific modules for international travel, road trips, and quick getaways separately.

What ages of kids does this cover?

Toddlers through teenagers. The age-specific guides are some of the most-used parts of the Blueprint because what works changes dramatically as kids get older.

We're on a tight budget — is this realistic for us?

Yes. The budget framework is built for families at different financial stages. There's no assumption you have a big travel fund — the whole point is showing you how to make it work with what you have.

Is this a course or a guide?

It's a practical blueprint — a combination of frameworks, guides, and templates you can actually use, not a course to watch and forget.
