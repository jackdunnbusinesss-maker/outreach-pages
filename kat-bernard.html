Common questions

Is this only for practicing PAs or is it relevant if I'm still in PA school?

Primarily for practicing PAs, but students in their final year get a lot out of the career architecture and contract negotiation content — those are things you'll use immediately after graduation.

What specialties are covered?

The frameworks are specialty-agnostic. Kat works with PAs in primary care, urgent care, surgery, dermatology, emergency medicine, and beyond — the career design principles apply across all of them.

I'm not burned out yet — is this still useful?

The best time to build these systems is before you hit the wall. Most people in the program wish they'd found it earlier. Prevention is the point.

Is this focused on moms only or is it for all PAs?

Kat's primary focus is PA moms, but working healthcare professionals who don't have children get value from the income, career design, and burnout modules too.

How much time does this require?

The program is built for people with almost no spare time. Core modules are designed to be completed in under 90 minutes. The community is there when you have five minutes or five hours.
