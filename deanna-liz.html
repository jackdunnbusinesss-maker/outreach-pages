Common questions

What age range is this for? I have toddlers / teens / one of each.

DeAnna covers travel across multiple kid ages. The frameworks apply broadly, and the destination-specific content often includes age-specific notes — because a trip with toddlers and a trip with teenagers are completely different experiences.

Is this domestic US travel or international?

Both. DeAnna covers domestic and international destinations. The planning framework works for either. The destination-specific content includes a mix of US and international locations.

Is this just for moms or for the whole family?

DeAnna's audience skews toward moms because she speaks to the person doing most of the planning — but the guide is for the whole trip and the whole family. Partners are welcome.

How is this different from just following travel blogs?

Travel blogs give you information. This gives you a system. The difference is that you don't spend five hours reading articles before every trip — you have a framework that works regardless of where you're going.

Does the food content account for picky eaters?

Yes. DeAnna is a foodie who has raised kids. The food section explicitly covers how to find places that satisfy the adults without everyone melting down because the menu has nothing recognizable for a seven-year-old.
