These are the things I hear from women who've been watching travel videos for years but still haven't gone.

"I've been planning this trip for two years. I still haven't booked it." The research loop is comfortable. It feels like progress. It isn't.
"I keep waiting for the timing to be right." The timing is never right. That's not a problem to solve — it's a story to drop.
"I don't want to go completely alone. I'm not sure I can handle it if something goes wrong." Solo doesn't mean unsupported. And things going wrong is actually smaller than you think.
