Questions

Is this for people who travel a lot, or casual travelers too?

Both. The system is built for frequent travelers, but the principles — and especially the anti-diet nutrition framework — are useful regardless of how often you leave home.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. The travel fitness system is specifically designed for the no-equipment reality of most travel. The home base program has gym options, but also works without one.

I love food — will I actually have to restrict myself?

No. The whole premise of the program is that restriction isn't sustainable. The framework teaches you how to eat what you enjoy without it becoming a problem for your health and fitness goals.

What fitness level do I need to be at?

Any level. The program scales. Whether you're just getting back into fitness or already training consistently, the workouts and nutrition approach are adapted to where you are.
