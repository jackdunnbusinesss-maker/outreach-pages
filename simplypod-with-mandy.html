If your POD shop isn't consistent, it's probably one of these five things

  • You're making designs based on what you like — not what's selling. Your shop looks good. The products just aren't moving. The gap between "looks great" and "converts" is market data.

  • You're uploading consistently but income swings wildly — some months $800, some months $150. There's no floor. No predictable base you can plan around.

  • You've jumped between niches trying to find what works — mugs, shirts, tote bags, sweatshirts — without a system for knowing whether a niche is actually viable before you spend weeks on it.

  • Your listings aren't converting even when people find them — you're getting views but not clicks, or clicks but not sales. The listing is leaking — and you don't know where.

  • You feel like you're always a step behind — by the time you find a trend, it's already saturated. The people making money from trends found them 6 weeks earlier. You need an early detection system, not a follow-the-leader approach.
