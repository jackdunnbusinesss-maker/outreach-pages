Questions before you join

What kind of business does this work for?

Service businesses, coaching, freelance, and product-based businesses. The systems principles apply broadly. The specifics are adapted based on your situation inside the program.

I'm just getting started — is this too advanced?

If you have some revenue coming in but feel stretched, this is the right time. The Constraint Map helps you identify where to focus even at an early stage.

How much time does this program take?

The irony of a time-management program taking too much time isn't lost on Erin. The core modules are concise. Most people implement as they go, so the learning and execution happen simultaneously.

Is there live coaching or is this self-paced?

Both. The core material is self-paced so you can work through it on your schedule. Live group coaching is available for people who want real-time feedback on their specific situation.
