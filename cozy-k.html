How this works

A Space Reset Built Around Your Life, Not a System

The reason most organization methods don't stick: they're built around generic rules, not your actual routine. Here's the process Kennedy uses — on her own spaces, and with her clients.

1

Start with the routine, not the room

Before touching anything, you figure out what you actually do in each space and what you need it to do for you. This step is why changes actually last.

2

One visible win first

You don't start with the whole apartment. You start with one corner, one drawer, one space you'll see every single day. That first win changes everything.

3

Build a reset that takes 20 minutes, not three hours

The goal isn't a space that looks perfect once. It's a space that's easy to come back to. The reset system is what makes it sustainable.

4

Layer in the aesthetic as you go

Once the function is right, you make it yours. Plants, lighting, textiles — but in an order that makes sense and doesn't create more chaos to manage.
