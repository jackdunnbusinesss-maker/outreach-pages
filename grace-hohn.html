Grace Hohn knows what it's like to love Pilates and hate what happens to your body when you're constantly on the move. She built her entire platform around one problem: how do you stay consistent with your fitness when your life doesn't stay in one place?

The Travel Pilates Method isn't a watered-down hotel-room workout. It's a structured approach to Pilates that adapts to wherever you are — a cramped Airbnb, an airport layover lounge, a beach house with no equipment. Grace has taught this to travelers and fitness enthusiasts who were tired of returning home from trips feeling like they had to start over.

If you're serious about your fitness but your schedule refuses to cooperate, this is the system that keeps you moving regardless.

What's inside the Travel Pilates Method

  • Foundation

    Core Pilates principles stripped of studio dependency — learn the movements that work everywhere, with or without a reformer or mat space.

  • Travel Sequences

    Pre-built 15, 25, and 45-minute sequences designed for small spaces. When you land after a red-eye, you know exactly what to do and for how long.

  • Adaptation System

    How to modify any Pilates movement for the space you actually have — hotel room, beach, hostel common room, wherever you end up.

  • Consistency Protocol

    The mental and scheduling framework that keeps you working out when you're in a different time zone, jet-lagged, and your brain is telling you to skip it.

  • Community

    A group of travelers and fitness enthusiasts who share routines, check in from different countries, and keep each other accountable across time zones.

Common questions

Do I need Pilates experience before joining?

No. The method starts with fundamentals. If you've done some Pilates before, you'll move through the foundation quickly. If you're new, you'll build it properly from the start.

What equipment do I need?

None required. A yoga mat is helpful but not necessary. The sequences are designed for the real conditions of travel — not an idealized studio setup.

How long are the workouts?

Sequences range from 15 to 45 minutes. Most travel days call for a 15-minute session. When you have more time, the longer sequences are there.

Is this just for constant travelers or does it work for people who travel occasionally?

Both. The method works whether you're traveling monthly or twice a year. It's about building a fitness approach that doesn't depend on your location — that's useful regardless of how often you travel.
