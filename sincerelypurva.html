The System

Here's what actually changes your results.

1
Build your Anki setup from scratch — for your specific subjects

Not a generic template. Your cards, for your exams, with the spacing intervals that actually stick. This takes two hours once and runs automatically after that.

2
Replace passive review with active recall — for every subject

Active recall is the only technique with real research behind it for long-term retention. It's not complicated — it's just that most people don't know how to build it into their actual study schedule.

3
Use the method that matches your exam type

Multiple choice exams need different prep than essay exams. Memorization-heavy subjects need different techniques than analysis-heavy ones. You've been using one method for everything — that's part of why it's not working.

4
Stay accountable until it's automatic

The group program has twice-weekly accountability calls. Most people don't need more information — they need to actually do it with someone else doing it alongside them.
