The Program

Six weeks. Real structure.
Built inside your actual life.

The Life Reset isn't a planner or an inspiration course. It's a framework that builds the architecture underneath your motivation — so when the rough week hits, you don't start from zero.

1

Your environment, reclaimed

Your space either signals possibility or overwhelm when you walk in. We fix that first — not with a renovation, but with a different relationship to what you already have.

2

A rhythm that actually fits

Not a 5am routine built for someone else's life. A daily rhythm designed around your real schedule, your real energy, and the actual city you live in.

3

Clarity on what you're building

Intentional living isn't abstract. It's specific. By week three you'll know exactly what you're moving toward and why — not a vision board, a direction.

4

A community doing it alongside you

Thirty women running the same six weeks at the same time. This is the part that makes the rest hold — not in theory, but because you're visible to people who are rooting for you.
