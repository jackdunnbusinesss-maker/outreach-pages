You're doing the work.
The brand isn't doing its job.

Posting consistently. Showing up. The content is real. But when the right client lands on your profile or website, something isn't clicking. Here's what that usually looks like.

You're explaining yourself constantly

Every sales conversation starts with you describing what you do. The brand should be doing that before they even reach out.

Inconsistent enquiries — or the wrong ones

The people who contact you either don't have budget, aren't ready, or aren't the right fit. The ones you want aren't finding you.

You're undercharging for what you do

You know you should charge more. But something about how the brand is positioned makes it hard to justify the price you want.

The brand doesn't feel like you

You built it, but it doesn't quite capture who you are or what you actually stand for. That disconnect shows — and it costs you the right clients.

Visibility anxiety is real

You don't want to "be the face" of everything. You want a brand that works without you turning into a persona you don't recognise.

You know what you want to build — you just can't see it clearly yet

The vision is there. The offer is there. The audience is growing. Something in the positioning is just slightly off, and you can't figure out what from the inside.

The fix

Brand positioning that
makes the right people say "this is for me."

This isn't about more content, better thumbnails, or a rebrand for the sake of it. It's about making your brand communicate exactly who you help, what you deliver, and why you're the right choice — in a way that resonates with the women who should be working with you.

  1. 1

    Positioning audit — we find exactly where the gap is

    I look at your brand with cold eyes — the homepage, the bio, the offer page, the content. I find the specific thing that's not landing and tell you exactly what it is.

  2. 2

    Positioning framework build — we define who you're for and why you

    Together we build the strategic foundation: the specific audience, the specific problem you solve, the specific reason they should choose you over anyone else. This becomes the filter for every content and marketing decision going forward.

  3. 3

    Messaging translation — the positioning becomes copy and content

    We take the framework and translate it into your actual brand: the headline copy, the bio, the offer page, the content angles. You walk away with words that work.

  4. 4

    Content strategy aligned to the new positioning

    Your content stops being random and starts reinforcing the same message every time. The right people see it and think: "this is exactly for me." The wrong people self-filter out.

  5. 5

    The right clients find you — consistently

    When positioning is right, inbound changes. Enquiries come from the right people. Explanation overhead drops. Conversion rates improve. You stop justifying your prices and start being paid them.
