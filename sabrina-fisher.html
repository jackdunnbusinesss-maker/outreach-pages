Questions

Is this therapy?

No — it's coaching. It's action-oriented and forward-facing. If you're in a place where you need clinical mental health support, Sabrina will be honest with you about that. But for most women in this program, what they need is a structured path and real accountability, which is exactly what this is.

I've tried coaches before and nothing changed. Why would this be different?

Because most coaching tells you what to do without addressing why you keep stopping. The Restart Cycle goes into the patterns themselves — not just the tactics on top of them.

How much time does this take per week?

The program is designed to fit around the real life of a busy woman or mom. Most participants engage for 2–3 hours a week between modules, community, and coaching sessions.

Do I need to be in a "rock bottom" moment to join?

Not at all. Many women who join are functioning fine from the outside and just feel like something is off. You don't need to be in crisis — you just need to want something different.
