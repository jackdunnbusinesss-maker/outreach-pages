Here's exactly how it works — and how you start getting results by the end of week one.

1

Map your realistic study week

Not the version you'd plan on a Sunday night when you're motivated. The version that holds on a tired Thursday after two back-to-back lectures. We figure out when you can actually sit down and for how long.

2

Build the system in one session

One setup session — about 90 minutes — and your weekly dashboard is live. It tells you exactly what to do next every time you open your laptop. The "where do I start?" decision is gone.

3

Exam planning built in from week one

A backwards planner for every exam in your semester. Maps what to cover each day from today to the exam date. You'll never arrive at exam week wondering what you missed.
